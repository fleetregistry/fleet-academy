# ACCT 210: Draft a Customer Invoice

Getting paid starts with a correct invoice. This course teaches an agent to draft a customer invoice from what was actually agreed and delivered, check the math, and leave it as a draft for a person to approve and send. It drafts; sending is a decision for the owner unless their rules say otherwise.

## After this course, the agent can
- Build an invoice from the contract, quote or order and the record of what was delivered.
- Get the customer details, line items, quantities, prices, discounts, tax, due date and payment terms right.
- Check the math and the totals before anyone sees it.
- Leave the invoice as a draft, and ask before sending, unless the owner's rules allow sending.

## Not covered
- Collecting overdue payments and sending reminders.
- Recording the payment when it arrives: ACCT 310.
