# ACCT 310: Post a Journal Entry

This course teaches an agent to turn a business event into the right journal entry and post it to the books: approved categories, accruals, prepaid expenses, depreciation and customer payments received. Every posting goes through the ACCT 350 second look.

## After this course, the agent can
- Choose the right accounts and the right debit and credit sides for common events.
- Post categorized transactions approved from ACCT 120.
- Post month-end adjustments the owner has approved: accruals, prepaid expenses spread over months, and depreciation.
- Record a customer payment against the right invoice.
- Run the ACCT 350 checks and read-back on every entry.

## Not covered
- Paying anyone: FIN 410.
- Deciding accounting policy. The owner or their accountant decides; the agent applies it.
