# Post a Journal Entry

Every entry in this course goes through **ACCT 350: Post With a Second Look**: preview, checks, approval, post, read-back.

## Debits and credits in one table
| Account type | Goes up with a | Goes down with a |
|---|---|---|
| Asset (cash, receivables, equipment) | debit | credit |
| Expense | debit | credit |
| Liability (bills owed, loans) | credit | debit |
| Revenue | credit | debit |
| Equity | credit | debit |

## Common entries
- **Approved categorization (from ACCT 120):** debit the expense, credit the bank or card account. A $87.50 Slack charge on the card is a debit to Software Subscriptions $87.50 and a credit to Company Card $87.50.
- **Customer payment received:** debit Business Checking, credit Accounts Receivable, applied to the specific invoice. If the payment is short or over, apply what was paid and flag the difference.
- **Accrual (expense incurred, not yet billed):** debit the expense, credit Accrued Liabilities, for the period it belongs to. Reverse it on the first day of the next period; when the bill arrives, post it normally.
- **Prepaid expense:** a $12,000 annual insurance payment is booked to Prepaid Insurance. Then each month, debit Insurance Expense $1,000 and credit Prepaid Insurance $1,000.
- **Depreciation:** use the schedule the owner or their accountant set. Never invent a useful life.

## Steps for every entry
1. Identify the event and its support document.
2. Draft the entry using the table above.
3. Run the ACCT 350 preview and checks.
4. Get approval if your rules require it.
5. Post.
6. Read back.
7. Record the entry ID (REC 101).

## When to stop and ask
- You're not sure which account or which side. Ask; don't guess.
- The entry is a policy choice (how long to depreciate, whether to accrue). Ask the owner or their accountant.
- Any ACCT 350 check fails.
