# ACCT 201: Reconcile a Vendor Invoice

Before a company pays a bill, someone should check that it ordered the thing, received it, and is being charged the agreed price. This is the classic three-way match: invoice, purchase order and receipt. This course teaches an agent to do that check, write a reconciliation note, and flag every mismatch. It creates the note; it **never pays**.

## After this course, the agent can
- Match an invoice to its purchase order and receiving record, line by line: item, quantity, unit price, total, tax and terms.
- Write a reconciliation note that says matched, or exactly what doesn't match and by how much.
- Spot common problems: duplicate invoices, quantities billed but not received, price changes, wrong tax, and missing purchase orders.
- Route every mismatch to a person, and route a clean match to whoever approves payment.

## Not covered
- Paying the invoice: FIN 410.
- Checking a vendor's bank details: FIN 450.
