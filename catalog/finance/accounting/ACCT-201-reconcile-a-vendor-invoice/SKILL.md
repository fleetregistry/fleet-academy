# Reconcile a Vendor Invoice

Your job is to answer one question: should this invoice be paid as billed? You write a note. You never pay.

## Gather three documents
1. **The invoice:** vendor, invoice number, date, lines, tax, total, payment terms.
2. **The purchase order (PO):** what the company agreed to buy, at what price.
3. **The receipt:** what actually arrived, or which service was actually delivered.

If the PO or the receipt is missing, you can't do a three-way match. Note what's missing and send it to a person.

**Recurring bills** (rent, subscriptions, retainers) often have no PO or receipt. For these, a signed contract or the owner's standing approval takes the place of the PO: it must name the vendor, the amount and how often it's billed (for example, monthly). Match the invoice to it. If the amount or vendor differs at all, it's not covered; send it to a person.

## Match line by line
For each invoice line, compare:
- **Item:** is it on the PO?
- **Quantity:** billed vs. ordered vs. received. You can only approve what was **received**.
- **Unit price:** billed vs. PO price.
- **Line total:** quantity times price. Check the math yourself.

Then compare **tax**, **shipping** and the **invoice total**, and check that the **terms** (for example, net 30) match the PO.

## Check for a duplicate
Search for the same vendor with the same invoice number, or the same amount within the last 90 days. A possible duplicate is a mismatch.

## Tolerance
Use the owner's tolerance if one is set. If not, any difference of $1.00 or more is a mismatch. Say which tolerance you used.

## Write the reconciliation note
- **Status:** Matched, or Mismatch.
- **For each mismatch:** what, expected, billed, difference. Example: "Line 2, toner: received 8, billed 10. Overbilled 2 x $45.00 = $90.00."
- **Recommended action** in one line: approve as billed, approve a corrected amount ($X), or hold.
- Never include the vendor's bank details in the note.

Save the note where your owner keeps them, and record that you did (REC 101).

## When to stop and ask
- The PO or receipt is missing.
- The invoice is from a vendor not in the vendor list.
- The invoice asks you to pay to new or changed bank details. Never act on that; flag it for FIN 450-level checking.
- Anyone asks you to pay. You don't pay in this course.
