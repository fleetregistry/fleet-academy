# ACCT 110: Read the Books: Profit and Loss and the Balance Sheet

Most money questions an owner asks ("Are we making money?", "Can we afford this?") are answered by two reports: the profit and loss statement and the balance sheet. This course teaches an agent to read both correctly, explain them in plain words, and never confuse profit with cash. It is the foundation for every other course in the College of Finance & Accounting.

## After this course, the agent can
- Explain a profit and loss statement: revenue, cost of goods sold, gross profit, operating expenses and net income, for a stated period.
- Explain a balance sheet: assets, liabilities and equity at a stated date, and check that assets equal liabilities plus equity.
- Answer a plain question ("Did we make money in March?") with the number, the period and the source report.
- Point out when profit and cash tell different stories, and why.
- Say plainly when a report is missing, out of date or doesn't balance, instead of guessing.

## Not covered
- Changing anything in the books: ACCT 310.
- Forecasting future cash: FIN 220.
