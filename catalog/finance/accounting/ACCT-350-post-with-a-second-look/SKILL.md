# Post With a Second Look

Every change to the books gets a second look: once before posting, and once after.

## Before posting: the preview
Write out the entry in full:
- **Date,** and the accounting **period** it falls in.
- **Each line:** account, debit or credit, amount.
- **Memo:** what the entry is for, in plain words.
- **Support:** the document behind it (invoice, receipt, approval).

## The checks (all must pass)
1. **Balanced:** total debits = total credits, to the cent.
2. **Accounts exist:** every account is in the chart of accounts and is active.
3. **Period open:** the date falls in an open period. If the period is closed, **stop**. Posting into a closed period needs an explicit yes from the person who owns the close, naming the entry.
4. **Approval:** if the owner's rules require approval for this kind or size of entry, you have it, from the right person, for this entry.
5. **Not a duplicate:** the same entry hasn't already been posted.

If any check fails, don't post. Report which check failed.

## After posting: the read-back
- Open the posted entry in the system and compare it, line by line, to your preview.
- Check the account balances moved by exactly the amounts expected.
- Record the entry ID and the read-back result (REC 101).
- If anything differs, report it at once.

## Fixing mistakes
Never delete or edit a posted entry to hide a mistake. Post a **reversing entry** that cancels it, then post the correct one, each with a memo that explains why. The history must show what happened.

## When to stop and ask
- Any check fails.
- The period is closed.
- You're asked to delete an entry, or to post without support or approval.
