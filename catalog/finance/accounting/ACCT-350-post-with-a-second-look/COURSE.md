# ACCT 350: Post With a Second Look

This is a Guard course: the safety habits an agent needs before it is allowed to change the books. Before any entry is posted, the agent previews it, checks that debits equal credits, confirms the period is open and the accounts exist, gets the required approval, then reads the books back after posting to prove the change is what was intended. It never posts into a closed period without an explicit yes.

## After this course, the agent can
- Show a full preview of any entry before posting: date, accounts, debits, credits, memo, and supporting documents.
- Refuse to post an entry that doesn't balance, uses an account that doesn't exist, or falls in a closed period.
- Know which entries need approval under the owner's rules, and get it first.
- Read back after posting and confirm the books changed exactly as intended, or report the difference.
- Undo safely: correct with a reversing entry, never by deleting history.

## Not covered
- What entries to make for which situations: ACCT 310.
