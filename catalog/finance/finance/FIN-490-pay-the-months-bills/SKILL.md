# Pay the Month's Bills

This is the whole job. Nothing here is new; it's every course in order, with no step skipped.

## 1. Collect
List every bill due this period. Include:
- invoices received,
- recurring bills (rent, subscriptions),
- anything already scheduled.

For each one: vendor, invoice number, amount, due date.

## 2. Reconcile (ACCT 201)
Run the three-way match on each bill. Recurring bills match against their contract or the owner's standing approval instead of a purchase order. Matched bills move on. Mismatches go to a **Held** list, each with its reason.

## 3. Check the cash (FIN 220)
Add up what's due. Compare it to the cash forecast. If paying everything would take cash below the owner's minimum, stop and show the owner the shortfall before scheduling anything. Deciding what to delay is the owner's call.

## 4. Get approvals, batched
Send the owner (or the approvers named in the rules) **one** approval request:
- each bill that needs approval: vendor, invoice, amount and due date,
- the total,
- the Held list, with reasons.

Bills covered by a standing approval (same vendor, same amount, same cadence) don't need a new yes; list them in the request as "paying under standing approval" so the owner sees them. Approval is for these bills at these amounts. A change in any amount needs a new yes.

## 5. Pay (FIN 410, with FIN 450 on every payment)
For each approved bill, on its timing:
- run all FIN 450 checks again, right before paying (things change),
- pay through the payment tool's stored vendor record,
- record the confirmation.

Any check that fails moves that bill to Held. The rest continue.

## 6. Post (ACCT 310 and ACCT 350)
Post each payment against its bill with the second look and read-back.

## 7. Report (COM 101)
Lead with the result: "Paid 18 bills, $42,310.00. 2 held. 1 needs you." Then three short lists:
- **Paid:** vendor, amount, confirmation number.
- **Held:** each bill and why.
- **Needs you:** the decisions only the owner can make.

## Never
- Pay a held bill because the run is "almost done."
- Treat the batch approval as approval for bills added after it.
- Type, store or repeat any account number.
