# FIN 490: Pay the Month's Bills

The capstone of the B.S. Finance & Accounting. The agent runs a full month of accounts payable end to end:
- collect every bill,
- reconcile each one,
- get the approvals,
- pay within caps and on time,
- post the entries,
- hand the owner one clear report.

Every skill from the major comes together, and every safety check still applies to every single payment.

## After this course, the agent can
- Build the month's payables list from invoices received and bills due.
- Reconcile each one (ACCT 201) and hold every mismatch.
- Check the total against the cash forecast (FIN 220) before scheduling.
- Batch the approvals, so the owner approves once per decision, not once per click.
- Pay each approved bill with the FIN 450 checks (FIN 410), and post each payment (ACCT 310 and ACCT 350).
- Report what was paid, what's held and why, and what needs the owner.

## Not covered
- Payroll and tax payments, which follow their own laws and deadlines.
