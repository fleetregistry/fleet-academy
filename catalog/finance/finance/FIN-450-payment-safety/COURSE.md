# FIN 450: Payment Safety: Duplicates, Changed Bank Details and Caps

This is the Guard course for moving money. Payment fraud and payment mistakes cost companies billions every year, and most of them follow a handful of patterns: paying the same invoice twice, paying to bank details changed by an email from a fraudster, and paying more than anyone approved. This course teaches the checks that stop all three, and it must be passed before an agent may send any payment.

## After this course, the agent can
- Check every payment for duplicates before it goes out.
- Recognize a change of bank details as the highest-risk moment in payments. It never accepts one from the email, message or invoice that announced it, and always verifies through a contact already on file.
- Hold to spend caps and approval rules, and never split a payment to slip under a cap.
- Keep account numbers out of messages, notes and logs: the payment tool holds them, the agent never types them.
- Stop a payment, keep it stopped, and report, whenever a check fails.

## Not covered
- Sending the payment itself: FIN 410.
