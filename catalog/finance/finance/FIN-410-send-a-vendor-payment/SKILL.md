# Send a Vendor Payment

Money that leaves rarely comes back. Every payment follows the same path, every time.

## Before you pay
1. **Reconciled:** the invoice passed ACCT 201 (matched, or approved at a corrected amount). Pay the reconciled amount, not the billed amount if they differ.
2. **FIN 450 checks pass:**
   - not a duplicate,
   - bank details unchanged or verified,
   - within caps,
   - approved for this payment,
   - no account numbers handled.
3. **Approval in hand,** when the rules need it. It names the vendor, invoice and amount. "Pay the bills" is not approval for a specific $8,000 payment.

## Timing
- Pay on or just before the due date, so cash stays in the company until it's needed.
- Pay early only when an early-payment discount was approved (for example, "2/10 net 30": 2% off if paid within 10 days). A $10,000 invoice paid in 10 days costs $9,800.
- Never pay late without telling the owner.

## Paying
- Use the payment tool's stored vendor record. You choose the vendor and invoice; the tool supplies the bank details. You never type or see the account number.
- Enter the amount and check it against the reconciled amount, to the cent.
- Add the invoice number to the payment memo so the vendor can match it.

## After paying
1. Get the payment tool's confirmation number.
2. Mark the invoice paid in the accounting system with the same amount and date, posting it the way ACCT 310 teaches, with the ACCT 350 checks.
3. Record it (REC 101): vendor, invoice, amount, date, confirmation number, who approved.

## If a payment fails or comes back
Don't retry blindly; you may end up paying twice. Report the failure and the reason, and wait for a person.

## When to stop and ask
- Any FIN 450 check fails.
- The approval doesn't match the payment (different amount or vendor).
- The payment tool asks you to type or confirm bank details.
