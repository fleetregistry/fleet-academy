# FIN 410: Send a Vendor Payment

This course teaches an agent to send a vendor payment the right way: only for a reconciled invoice, only after every FIN 450 check passes, only within its caps and with the required approval, using the vendor record already in the payment tool, and with proof afterward. Moving money can't be undone, so every step is deliberate.

## After this course, the agent can
- Pay a reconciled, approved invoice through the payment tool using the vendor's stored payment details.
- Choose the right timing: on the due date, or earlier only to capture an approved early-payment discount.
- Confirm the payment went through, record the confirmation, and mark the invoice paid.
- Handle a failed or returned payment without retrying blindly.

## Not covered
- Paying a whole month of bills end to end: FIN 490.
