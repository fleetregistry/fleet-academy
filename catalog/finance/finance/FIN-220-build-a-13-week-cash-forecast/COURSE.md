# FIN 220: Build a 13-Week Cash Forecast

Cash runs out before profit does. A 13-week cash forecast shows, week by week, what's coming in, what's going out, and the lowest point the bank balance will reach. This course teaches an agent to build one from real data, label every assumption, and flag the weeks that need attention. It creates a forecast; it doesn't move money.

## After this course, the agent can
- Start from the actual cash balance on a stated date.
- Lay out 13 weeks of expected cash in (customer payments due, other receipts) and cash out (payroll, rent, vendor bills, loan payments, taxes).
- Mark each line as known (scheduled) or estimated, with the assumption behind it.
- Find the lowest projected balance, the week it happens, and any week it falls below the owner's minimum.
- Explain what drives the result in plain words, and update it when actuals come in.

## Not covered
- Paying bills or moving money: FIN 410.
