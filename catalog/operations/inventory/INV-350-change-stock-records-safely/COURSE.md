# INV 350: Change Stock Records Safely: Cutoffs, Recounts and Reversals

This is the Guard course for changing inventory records. A wrong stock adjustment is quiet and expensive: it hides theft, triggers orders nobody needs, or makes stock vanish that is sitting on the shelf. Most bad adjustments follow a few patterns: comparing a count with a system figure from a different moment, adjusting a big difference on one person's count, "fixing" history by deleting it, and posting without saying what will change. This course teaches the checks that stop them, and must be passed before an agent may change a stock record.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.h.7 Maintain inventory records.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.4.g.14 Analyze data to identify or resolve operational problems.

## After this course, the agent can
- Compare a count with the system figure as it stood when the count was taken, allowing for receipts and picks since.
- Hold a large difference for a recount by a different person and the approval the owner's rules require.
- Post one adjustment per item with a reason, after reading back exactly what will change.
- Correct a wrong transaction with a reversing entry, never by deleting history.
- Check the record after the change, and report.

## Not covered
- Working through a whole count sheet: INV 310.
