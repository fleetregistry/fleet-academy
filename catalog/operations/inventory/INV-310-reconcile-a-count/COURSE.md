# INV 310: Reconcile a Count

A count sheet comes back with differences on a dozen lines, and the easy thing is to adjust every line to match the count. That turns a misplaced pallet into a write-off on one bin and phantom stock on the next, turns a counter who wrote units instead of cases into a loss, and buries a theft under routine adjustments. This course teaches an agent to work through a count the way a good inventory controller does: explain each difference before touching it, fix misplacements as moves, recount what's in doubt, adjust only what's left within its limits, and send the rest to a person.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.3.g.2 Inventory materials or equipment.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.h.7 Maintain inventory records.

## After this course, the agent can
- Sort every line of a count into matched, explained, to recount, to adjust, and to escalate.
- Recognize a shortage in one location and the same overage nearby as a misplacement, and move it instead of writing it off.
- Catch counts written in the wrong unit.
- Trust a recount by a second person over a first count that disagrees with it.
- Send serialized or high-value shortages to a person before any write-off.
