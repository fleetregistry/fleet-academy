# INV 450: Purchase Order Safety: Duplicates, Prices, Suppliers and Limits

This is the Guard course for buying. A purchase order commits the company's money the moment a supplier accepts it, and the costly mistakes are predictable: ordering again what is already on its way, accepting a price the company never agreed to, buying from a supplier nobody has vetted, and slipping past a spending limit one order at a time. This course teaches the checks that stop those, and must be passed before an agent may place a purchase order.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.3.e.3 Purchase materials, equipment, or other resources.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.a.2.b.5 Monitor organizational procedures to ensure proper functioning.

## After this course, the agent can
- Check open and in-transit orders before buying, so a reorder alert doesn't double an order.
- Hold an order when the supplier's price differs from the agreed price, and tell the owner the difference.
- Buy only from approved suppliers, using their details on file.
- Check both the per-order and the monthly spending limits, and never split an order to fit under one.
- Stop, keep the order unplaced, and report when any check fails.

## Not covered
- Building and placing the order itself: INV 410.
- Paying the supplier's bill: FIN 410.
