# INV 110: Read the Stock Picture

"How many do we have?" has at least four honest answers: on hand, available, on hold and on order. Agents most often answer with the biggest number on the screen, in whatever unit it happens to be in, and miss that half of it is already promised to other orders. This course teaches an agent to read an inventory record correctly: what can actually be used, in the unit the question needs, how long it will last, and how fresh the count behind it is. It only reads.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.3.g.4 Monitor inventories of products or materials.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.3.g.1 Monitor availability of equipment or supplies.

## After this course, the agent can
- Work out available stock: on hand, minus what's allocated to open orders, minus what's on hold.
- Convert between cases and units before comparing a need with stock.
- Say when stock runs out at the current rate of use, and whether the next receipt lands in time.
- Say how old the count behind a figure is.
- Decline to change a stock record, and send a reported difference to the right process.

## Not covered
- Changing stock records: INV 350 and INV 310.
- Ordering more: INV 410.
