# INV 210: Receive a Shipment

The receipt is where the stock record and the bill both start. If an agent receives what the packing slip says instead of what the dock counted, the company has stock it can't find and pays for goods it never got. This course teaches an agent to create the receipt record for a delivery: the counted good quantity against the right purchase order, damage and shortages written down separately, unordered goods held instead of quietly added, and the buyer told about every difference.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.1.e.1 Receive shipments.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.b.2.j.3 Inspect shipments to ensure correct order fulfillment.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.h.1 Record details of deliveries or shipments.

## After this course, the agent can
- Receive the quantity that was counted in good condition, not the quantity on the packing slip or the order.
- Record damaged and missing goods separately, and leave the order open for what didn't come.
- Convert between the order's unit and the slip's unit before calling anything short or over.
- Match a delivery to the purchase order it names.
- Hold goods that aren't on any order, and ask the buyer.

## Not covered
- Changing stock that's already on the record: INV 350 and INV 310.
- Disputing the bill with the supplier: ACCT 201.
