# INV 490: Restock End to End

The capstone of the B.S. Operations. One real job, done from start to finish: a site is running low on something it needs, and the agent gets it back in stock. That means reading the stock picture correctly (INV 110), passing every purchase-order check (INV 450), placing the order and reading its acknowledgement (INV 410), booking the delivery into the dock (LOG 210), receiving what actually arrives (INV 210), and closing the loop with the owner, stopping wherever a person has to decide.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.3.a.3 Manage inventories of products or organizational resources.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.3.e.1 Order materials, supplies, or equipment.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.1.e.1 Receive shipments.

## After this course, the agent can
- Take a low-stock alert to stock on the shelf without skipping a step or an approval.
- Show proof at each step: the stock figures, the checks passed, the order and its acknowledgement, the booking, the receipt.
- Stop where the rules say a person decides, and pick up cleanly after.
- Finish with the order closed or left open for exactly what's still owed, and the owner told.

## Not covered
- Paying the supplier's bill: FIN 410.
