# INV 410: Place a Purchase Order

A purchase order is a small contract: once the supplier accepts it, the company owes the money. This course teaches an agent to build and place one correctly: the right quantity from the reorder rule, rounded to how the supplier sells, the agreed price, the right delivery address and date, inside its limits, and then to read the supplier's acknowledgement, because suppliers often accept an order with a different price or date and say so only there.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.3.e.1 Order materials, supplies, or equipment.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.3.e.4 Purchase stocks of merchandise or supplies.

## After this course, the agent can
- Work out the order quantity from the owner's reorder rule, counting stock on order.
- Round it to the supplier's pack size and minimum order, in the supplier's unit.
- Put the right ship-to site and needed-by date on the order.
- Prepare an order that's over its limit for the right person's approval instead of placing it.
- Compare the supplier's acknowledgement with the order and report any change before treating it as confirmed.

## Not covered
- The safety checks before any order: INV 450.
- Receiving what arrives: INV 210.
