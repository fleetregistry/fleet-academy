# LOG 210: Book a Delivery

Booking a delivery looks like picking a time, but most failed deliveries trace back to something the booking missed: the slot was in the wrong time zone, the site has no loading dock, the dock was already taken, or the customer was told "confirmed" before the carrier agreed. This course teaches an agent to book a new delivery or dock appointment that will actually work, and to ask before anything that adds a charge or changes someone else's booking.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.b.5.b.1 Schedule product or material transportation.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.b.4.j.29 Arrange delivery of goods or services.

## After this course, the agent can
- Book a delivery inside the receiving site's hours, in the site's own time zone, and tell each party the time in theirs.
- Check how the goods will come off the truck (dock, liftgate, equipment) before booking.
- Avoid double-booking a dock door, given how long each unload takes.
- Keep "requested" and "confirmed" apart when telling a customer.
- Ask the owner before any extra charge, and before moving or cancelling an existing booking.

## Not covered
- Finding out where a shipment is: LOG 110.
- Receiving the goods when they arrive: INV 210.
