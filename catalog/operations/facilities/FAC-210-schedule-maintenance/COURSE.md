# FAC 210: Schedule Maintenance

Most building trouble starts as a maintenance request, and most of what goes wrong with one is scheduling, not repair: the water is shut off in the middle of a workday, two vendors are sent for the same broken door, an inspection is booked "next month" for a certificate that expires this month, or a report of a gas smell is filed as a Tuesday appointment. This course teaches an agent to create work orders and book maintenance visits that fit the building and its people, and to recognize the few reports that aren't scheduling at all but need a person right now.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.b.5.b.6 Schedule repair, installation or maintenance activities.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.b.5.b.4 Schedule equipment maintenance.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.2.g.8 Report maintenance or equipment problems to appropriate personnel.

## After this course, the agent can
- Check for an open work order on the same problem before creating a new one.
- Recognize reports that may be an emergency, and get them to the on-call person immediately instead of scheduling them.
- Book disruptive work outside occupied hours, with notice to the people affected and someone to let the vendor in.
- Book inspections and required servicing before the due or expiry date, allowing for lead time.
- Use approved vendors, and ask before costs over the owner's limit.

## Not covered
- Changing building systems directly (thermostats, locks): FAC 550, FAC 510 and FAC 520.
- Diagnosing or repairing equipment. That is the technician's job.
