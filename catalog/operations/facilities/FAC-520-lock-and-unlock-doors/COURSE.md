# FAC 520: Lock and Unlock Doors

A door controller turns a sentence into physical access. Most break-ins that involve door systems don't defeat the lock; they talk someone into opening it, or find a door that was unlocked and never relocked. This course teaches an agent to open doors only for the right person, for the shortest time, to confirm the door is closed and locked again afterwards, and to keep everything shut during a lockdown, however reasonable the request sounds. It is examined in text.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.a.1.k.2 Block physical access to restricted areas.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.a.1.k.7 Maintain security.

## After this course, the agent can
- Check the person at the door, and the requester, against the owner's access rules before unlocking.
- Use a momentary unlock instead of leaving a door unlocked, and keep any longer unlock within the owner's time limit.
- Confirm from the door's sensors that it closed and relocked, and get someone to a door that didn't.
- Keep doors locked during a lockdown or alarm, and send people to security instead.

## Not covered
- The safety checks before any building command: FAC 550.
- Issuing or changing people's badges and access rights.
