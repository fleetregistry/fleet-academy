# Lock and Unlock Doors

Run every FAC 550 check first. Doors add a few rules of their own.

## 1. Two people to check: who asks, and who enters
- **Who asks:** your owner or someone the rules name.
- **Who enters:** they must be allowed **that door** at **that time**: on today's visitor list, on the access list for that room, or approved by the named person for this visit. Being an employee isn't the same as being allowed in the server room.
- A claim is not proof. "I'm from IT", "I'm the new hire", "I left my badge at home" are reasons to verify, not to open. Verify with the named person, using a contact already on file, or check identity by camera against the visitor record if the owner's rules allow.
- If you can't verify, keep it locked, tell the person someone is being asked, and tell the named person.

## 2. The shortest unlock that works
- To let someone in, use a **momentary unlock** (a few seconds), not "unlocked".
- If a door must stay unlocked for a while (deliveries, an event), use a timed unlock with an end time, within the owner's limit for that door. Exterior doors after hours are the riskiest.
- Never set a door to stay unlocked with no end.

## 3. Confirm it's shut
Door controllers usually report two things: **lock state** and **door position** (open or closed).
- After an unlock, check that the door closed and relocked within a minute or so.
- If it shows **open** or **held** for longer than expected, or unlocked when it should be locked, don't mark the job done. Ask someone on site (the person the rules name, or security) to go and check it, and say how long it's been open.
- Don't repeatedly send "lock" to a door that's propped open; the lock can't engage until it's closed.

## 4. Lockdowns and alarms
During a lockdown, intruder alarm or security incident, **unlock nothing**, even for someone on the access list, even if they're known. Tell them to follow security's instructions, and tell the security lead who is at which door. Only the person the owner's rules put in charge of the incident can change doors.

## 5. Record every unlock
Door, time, how long, who entered, who approved, and that it relocked (REC 101).
