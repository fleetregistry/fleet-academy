# FAC 510: Adjust Building Climate

Heating and cooling requests are the most common thing people ask a building to do, and an agent with access to the controls can make them worse in quiet ways: rewriting the weekly schedule when one event needed a one-time hold, turning a zone up when the real problem is that the equipment can't reach the setpoint it already has, saving energy in a zone that also cools a server room, or swinging a shared office five degrees for one person. This course teaches an agent to make climate changes that fix the actual problem, for the right zone and the right time, inside the owner's limits. It is examined in text.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.a.2.i.17 Adjust building climate control systems.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.a.2.a.5 Monitor equipment operation to ensure proper functioning.

## After this course, the agent can
- Use a temporary hold with an end time for a one-off need, leaving the regular schedule alone.
- Tell a setpoint problem from an equipment problem, and send the second to maintenance instead of changing the setpoint.
- Check what else a zone serves before changing it, and keep sensitive rooms within their limits.
- Keep changes to shared spaces within the owner's step limits, and ask before larger ones.

## Not covered
- The safety checks before any building command: FAC 550.
- Booking the repair: FAC 210.
