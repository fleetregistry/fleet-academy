# Adjust Building Climate

Run every FAC 550 check first (fresh state, who asked, limits, read-back, undo, check after). This course is about choosing the right change.

## 1. Find the zone, and everything it serves
A thermostat controls a **zone**, and a zone can cover more than its name suggests. Check the zone's list of rooms or the notes on it before changing anything.
- If a zone includes a server room, a lab, cold storage, a medicine fridge or anything with its own temperature limit, that limit comes first. A change that would break it is a stop: tell the owner, and offer a change that keeps the sensitive room in range (or that the zones be split by a technician).

## 2. Setpoint or equipment?
Compare the **reading** (actual temperature) with the **setpoint** (target).
- If the room is far from its setpoint (more than about 2°F or 1°C) for more than an hour or so, the equipment isn't keeping up. Raising or lowering the setpoint further won't fix it. Don't change the setpoint; create a maintenance request (FAC 210) with the zone, the reading, the setpoint and how long it's been off, and tell the person who complained.
- If the room is at its setpoint and people are still uncomfortable, then a setpoint change may be the answer.

## 3. One-off need: a temporary hold, not a schedule edit
For an event, a late meeting, or a weekend visit:
- Use a **temporary hold** or override with an **end time**, for that zone only, starting a little before people arrive (about 30 minutes) and ending when they leave.
- Don't edit the weekly schedule or set a permanent hold for a one-time need. Those outlive the reason and waste energy or leave people cold weeks later.
- Change the regular schedule only when the owner asks for a lasting change.

## 4. Shared spaces: small steps
One person's "freezing" is another person's "fine".
- Keep changes in shared zones within the owner's step limit (if none is stated, 2°F or 1°C at a time).
- A larger change needs the person the owner names. Tell the requester what you did and what would need approval.

## 5. Within the allowed range
Never set a value outside the owner's allowed range, and never set heating above cooling.

## Report
Say what changed (zone, from, to, until when), or why nothing changed and what happens next.
