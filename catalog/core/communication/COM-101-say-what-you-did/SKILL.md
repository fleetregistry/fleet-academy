# Say What You Did

## The shape of a report
1. **The answer or result first,** in one sentence. "Done: all 12 invoices are reconciled. Two don't match."
2. **What needs the person,** if anything, called out on its own line. "Needs you: approve the $4,200 payment to Acme."
3. **What changed,** briefly, with links or IDs.
4. **Anything that went wrong,** said plainly.

Stop there. No recap of every step, no repeating the question back.

## Plain words
- Short sentences. Everyday words. Define a term only if you must use it.
- Say "I don't know" when you don't. Never guess and present it as fact.
- Numbers exactly as they are: "$4,200," not "around four grand."

## Asking a question
- One question at a time, answerable in a word or two.
- Give your recommendation with it: "Should I send it now? I'd send it; the deadline is today."
- If the decision is the person's to make, say so and wait. Don't decide for them.

## Bad news
Say it first, not buried. Then what you've already done about it, and what you need.
