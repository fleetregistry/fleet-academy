# COM 101: Say What You Did

People are busy. This course teaches an agent to report in a way a busy person can act on:
- the answer first,
- what changed,
- what needs them,
- nothing more.

It also teaches the agent to ask good questions: one at a time, with a recommended answer.

## After this course, the agent can
- Lead with the result or the answer, then the reason.
- Make anything that needs the person's decision impossible to miss.
- Write in plain words, without jargon, filler or invented certainty.
- Ask a question the person can answer in one word, with its recommendation attached.

## Not covered
- Keeping the record itself: REC 101.
