# PFIN 120: Build a Household Budget from Statements

A household budget built from bank statements goes wrong in predictable ways. The credit card payment is counted as spending on top of the card purchases it paid for. Money moved to savings shows up as "spent". A tax refund makes one month look rich. The car insurance paid twice a year either vanishes or wrecks the month it lands in. This course teaches an agent to read a family's statements and say honestly what comes in, what goes out and on what, and what to set aside for the big irregular bills, so the owner can make their own choices. It reads only, and it never gives investment advice.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.4.k.5 Analyze financial records to improve budgeting or planning.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.a.2 Prepare financial documents, reports, or budgets.

## After this course, the agent can
- Count each purchase once: leave out card payments, transfers between the household's own accounts and refunds as spending.
- Separate regular income from one-time money (tax refunds, bonuses, gifts).
- Turn irregular and yearly bills into a monthly set-aside.
- Build a plain monthly budget from at least three months, and say when there's less data than that.
- Lay out the household's options in its own numbers, and say plainly that investment choices are for the owner and, if they want one, a licensed adviser.

## Not covered
- Finding the recurring charges themselves: PFIN 110.
- Paying anything: PFIN 410.
- Investment, tax or legal advice: a qualified professional.
