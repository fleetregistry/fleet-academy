# PFIN 410: Pay a Household Bill

Paying a family's bill looks simple, and the mistakes are small and expensive: a payment sent on the due date that arrives three days late, a "past due" amount paid again when the last payment simply crossed in the mail, a retried payment that goes through twice, a "paid" message when the payment is only scheduled, a card number read out over the kitchen table and typed into a portal. This course teaches an agent to pay one household bill within the owner's limits, on time, once, and with proof, after every PFIN 450 check has passed.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.1.c.7 Pay charges, fees, or taxes.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.j.3 Maintain financial or account records.

## After this course, the agent can
- Pay the right amount: the current amount due, and a past-due amount only after checking it wasn't already paid.
- Time the payment so it arrives by the due date, counting the bank's delivery time, weekends and holidays.
- Pay through the saved payee or the biller's own account, and leave card details for the owner to enter.
- Get a confirmation, say "scheduled" or "paid" accurately, and record it.
- Handle a failed or unclear payment without paying twice.

## Not covered
- The safety checks themselves: PFIN 450.
- Medical bills before insurance has processed them: HLTH 110.
- Paying all of a month's bills: HOME 490.
