# Pay a Household Bill

Run every PFIN 450 check first. Then follow these steps.

## 1. The right amount
- Pay the **amount due on the current bill**, unless the owner's rule says otherwise (for example "pay credit cards in full").
- **A past-due amount** on the bill: before paying it, look at the bank's payments since the bill was issued. A payment made around the bill date often crosses the bill in the mail. If last month's amount already left the account, don't pay it again: pay only the new charges, and tell the owner the bill shows the old amount as unpaid so they can confirm with the biller.
- Fees or charges you can't explain: hold and ask.
- Medical bills: don't pay one that insurance hasn't processed yet (see HLTH 110).

## 2. The right time
- The bill pay screen says how long a payment takes to arrive (often 1 to 3 business days electronically, longer for a mailed check). Count business days back from the due date, skipping weekends and holidays, and schedule it to arrive on or before the due date.
- If it can't arrive in time, tell the owner now, and say what you'd suggest (paying on the biller's own site may post the same day).
- Not much earlier than needed, unless the owner prefers. Never late without telling the owner.

## 3. The right way
- Use the payee saved in the bank's bill pay, or the biller's account the owner set up, from the account the owner named for bills.
- **If a payment page asks for a card number, expiry, security code, bank login or password, stop.** The owner enters those on that page, or saves a payment method there. You never type, read out or repeat them, even if the owner offers to tell you. Tell the owner exactly where to enter them, and offer to finish and confirm the payment once they have.
- Enter the amount and check it to the cent before submitting. Put the account or bill reference in the memo if the payee needs it.

## 4. Proof
- Get the confirmation number.
- Say exactly what happened: "scheduled for March 3, to arrive by March 5" is not "paid". Call it paid only when it has left the account or the biller shows it.
- Update the household's bills list and record it (REC 101): biller, amount, from which account (by nickname), date sent, expected arrival, confirmation number, who approved or which standing rule allowed it.

## 5. If it fails or you can't tell
A page that times out, an error after you pressed pay, no confirmation: **don't press pay again.** First look at the bank's pending payments and the biller's account to see whether it went through. If it did, stop. If it clearly didn't, and you're still within the rules, you may try once more. If you can't tell, stop and tell the owner what you see.

## Stop and ask when
- Any PFIN 450 check fails.
- The amount is over the owner's limit or doesn't match what was approved.
- A page asks for card or bank details.
- The payment can't arrive by the due date.
