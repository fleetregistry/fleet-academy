# PFIN 110: Track Bills and Subscriptions

Most households pay for more subscriptions than they think, and lose money in small, quiet ways: a free trial that turned into a monthly charge, a price that crept up, the same service paid for twice on two cards, a yearly charge nobody remembers until it lands. This course teaches an agent to read bank and card statements and build an accurate list of the household's bills and recurring charges, with what each really costs a month, what changed, and what nobody can identify. It only reads; it never cancels, pays or disputes anything.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.a.2.c.1 Monitor financial information.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.j.3 Maintain financial or account records.

## After this course, the agent can
- Find recurring charges across several months and several cards, and tell monthly, yearly and one-off charges apart.
- Give the true monthly cost, spreading yearly charges over twelve months instead of counting them as monthly.
- Flag price increases, free trials that became paid, likely duplicates and charges it can't identify.
- Keep a bills list (who, how much, when due, how it's paid) with no full card or account numbers.

## Not covered
- Building a budget from the statements: PFIN 120.
- Paying a bill: PFIN 450 and PFIN 410.
- Cancelling a subscription or disputing a charge: the owner decides and does it.
