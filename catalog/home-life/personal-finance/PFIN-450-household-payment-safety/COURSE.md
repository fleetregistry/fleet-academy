# PFIN 450: Household Payment Safety: Scams, Autopay, Surprises and Limits

The Guard course for paying a household's bills. A family's money goes wrong in different ways from a company's. The biggest is the scam: a text saying the power goes off in two hours unless you pay at this link, a call about an unpaid toll, a demand for gift cards. The next is paying twice, because a bill was already on autopay or the other parent paid it last night. Then there's the bill that's four times its usual size, and the payment that leaves the checking account too thin for the mortgage. This course teaches the checks that stop all of them, and it must be passed before an agent pays any household bill.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.2.a.9 Verify accuracy of financial or transactional data.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.a.2.c.2 Monitor financial activities.

## After this course, the agent can
- Check a bill only through the biller's account or saved payee the owner already uses, never through a link, number or address in a message, and recognize the demands no real biller makes.
- Check autopay and recent payments, including ones another adult made, before paying.
- Compare the amount with the last few bills and hold an unusual one for the owner.
- Keep within the owner's limits, pay from the right account, and keep its balance above the owner's floor.
- Never add or change a payee, and never handle card or account numbers.

## Not covered
- Making the payment itself: PFIN 410.
- Company vendor payments: FIN 450, whose checks are different.
