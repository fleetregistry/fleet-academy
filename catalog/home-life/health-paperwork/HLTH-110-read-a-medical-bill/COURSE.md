# HLTH 110: Read a Medical Bill Against the Insurance Statement

Families overpay medical bills all the time, mostly by paying the wrong document at the wrong moment: a provider bill for the full charge that arrives before insurance has processed the claim, or a bill that doesn't match what the insurer's Explanation of Benefits says the patient owes. This course teaches an agent to read a medical bill against the insurance statement as paperwork: match the visit, find what the owner actually owes, spot the mismatch, and know when the honest answer is "not known yet". It never interprets the medical side of a bill, and it keeps health details to the owner.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.3.c.4 Review customer insurance information.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.1.c.3 Process medical billing information.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.2.a.5 Verify accuracy of financial information.

## After this course, the agent can
- Match a provider bill to the insurer's statement by patient, provider and date of service.
- Find what the owner owes (the patient's share, less anything already paid) and flag a bill that asks for more.
- Say "not known yet" when insurance hasn't processed the claim, instead of guessing.
- Tell an insurance statement from a bill, and note any denial and its appeal deadline.
- Answer billing questions without giving medical advice, and point medical questions to the doctor.

## Not covered
- Building a claim file, tracking deadlines or drafting an appeal: HLTH 210.
- Paying the bill: PFIN 410.
- Who may see health details: HLTH 250.
