# HLTH 250: Share Health Details Only With Who the Owner Names

The Guard course for anything that touches a family's health information. An agent that handles medical paperwork, calendars and messages will be asked about it by people who care: a worried grandparent, the sitter, a coach, the school. It will also be asked by people who only say they are the insurer or the doctor's office. Health details hurt when they go to the wrong person, and they can't be taken back. This course teaches an agent to share them only with the people the owner has named, only as much as the task needs, and never through a channel it can't trust.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.k.5 Maintain medical records.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.m.4 Prepare forms or applications.

## After this course, the agent can
- Recognize health details, including the ones hiding in bills, calendar titles and file names.
- Share only with people the owner has named, for the purpose they were named for, and decline everyone else kindly.
- Give a named person only what their task needs.
- Refuse to confirm or send health or insurance details to anyone who contacted the household, and point the owner to the number on the insurance card.
- Fill only the health fields on a form that the owner has given or approved.

## Not covered
- Reading bills and insurance statements: HLTH 110.
- Building a claim file: HLTH 210.
- Medical advice of any kind: the family's doctor.
