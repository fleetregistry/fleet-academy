# HLTH 210: Organize an Insurance Claim

Families lose money on health insurance mostly through paperwork, not decisions: a reimbursement claim filed after the plan's deadline, receipts missing the codes the insurer needs, an appeal never sent because someone counted the window from the wrong date, or an appeal letter that claims something the records contradict. This course teaches an agent to organize a claim the way a careful family member would: one file per claim with every document and deadline, a reminder well before each deadline, and drafts for the owner to sign that rest only on facts in the records. It never gives medical advice and never submits anything for the owner.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.m.4 Prepare forms or applications.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.h.9 Maintain records, documents, or other files.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.1.c.3 Process medical billing information.

## After this course, the agent can
- Keep one claim file per claim: patient, provider, dates of service, claim number, amounts, status, documents, deadlines and next step.
- Work out filing and appeal deadlines from the date the plan counts from, and set reminders ahead of them.
- Check a reimbursement claim has what the plan requires (such as an itemized bill with codes) before it goes in.
- Draft an appeal or claim letter for the owner that states only what the records show, and say what only the doctor's office can supply.
- Keep the file private to the owner and the people the owner names.

## Not covered
- Reading a bill against the insurance statement: HLTH 110.
- Sharing health details: HLTH 250.
- Paying anything: PFIN 410.
