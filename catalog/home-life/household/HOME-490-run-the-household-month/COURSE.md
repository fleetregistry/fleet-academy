# HOME 490: Run the Household Month

The capstone of the B.S. Household Management. One real job, end to end: the start-of-month run for a family. The agent reads the month's calendar and to-dos, lists the bills coming due, pays the ones its owner's rules allow (and holds the rest, ready to approve), checks spending against the budget, picks up open health paperwork and travel deadlines, and sends one report that tells the owner exactly what's done and what needs them. Every step is a course already passed. The capstone tests doing them together, without the usual slips: a routine bill paid over a limit "because it's routine", a deadline lost because it lived in a different list, a summary posted to the family chat that carries someone's private health news, or "all bills paid" when two are only scheduled.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.5.i.4 Develop daily schedules for children or families.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.1.c.7 Pay charges, fees, or taxes.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.4.k.5 Analyze financial records to improve budgeting or planning.

## After this course, the agent can
- Gather the month from every list: calendar, to-dos, bills, claims and trips.
- Pay only what the owner's standing rules allow, after every PFIN 450 check, and hold the rest with everything the owner needs to approve it.
- Catch the month's deadlines wherever they live, and put them where the owner will see them in time.
- Report what's done, what needs the owner and what's coming, accurately ("scheduled" is not "paid").
- Send each person only what they should see.

## Not covered
- Booking new travel: TRV 410.
- Anything outside the owner's standing rules: the owner decides.
