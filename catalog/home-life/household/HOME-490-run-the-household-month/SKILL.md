# Run the Household Month

Nothing here is new. It's the courses you've passed, done together, with nothing dropped between them.

## 1. Gather the month
Collect everything due or happening this month, from every place it lives:
- the family calendar and to-do list (HOME 110): conflicts, gaps, deadlines;
- bills coming due (PFIN 110): biller, amount, due date, autopay or by hand;
- open health paperwork (HLTH 210): claims to file, appeals, bills waiting on insurance, with their deadlines;
- trips (TRV 210): cancellation deadlines, check-in dates, documents to renew.
Make one dated list. A deadline in one list is still the owner's deadline.

## 2. Bills: pay, hold or leave
For each bill:
- **On autopay or already paid** (by anyone): leave it; note it.
- **Within the owner's standing rules:** run every PFIN 450 check (real bill, not paid, normal amount, within limits and the account's floor, saved payee), then pay it on time (PFIN 410).
- **Outside the rules** (over the limit, a bill the owner always approves, an unusual amount, a failed check): **hold it.** Don't pay it because it's routine, small, or due soon. Put it in the owner's approval list with biller, amount, due date, account, and why it's held.
- Medical bills wait for the insurance statement (HLTH 110).
Check the account floor against the whole month's payments, not one bill at a time.

## 3. Budget check
Compare last month's spending with the budget (PFIN 120). Note categories well over, and irregular bills coming that need their set-aside. Options, not advice.

## 4. Fix what you're allowed to
Add the month's new items (HOME 210). For conflicts, propose the fix; change existing entries only within HOME 350's checks.

## 5. One report to the owner
1. **Needs you:** each decision, with what's needed to make it in one reply (the bills to approve with amounts and dates, conflicts to settle, deadlines that need the owner's action).
2. **Done:** what you paid or scheduled, each with amount and confirmation, and its true state ("scheduled to arrive November 17" is not "paid").
3. **Coming up:** the month's dates and deadlines, soonest first.
Don't claim anything done that you didn't confirm.

## 6. Who sees what
The full report goes to the owner. If the owner wants a family version (a group chat, a fridge list), it carries only what everyone should see: plans, chores, pickups. Leave out each person's health details, money matters and anything the owner said is private, unless the owner says otherwise.

## Record
Record every payment, change and message (REC 101).

## Never
- Pay a held bill to finish the run.
- Treat "run the month" as approval for anything outside the standing rules.
- Give medical, legal or investment advice.
