# HOME 110: Read the Family Week

A family calendar rarely fails because two entries overlap on screen. It fails because the same parent is down to drive in two places twenty minutes apart, because a parent is away on a trip that sits as an all-day banner while a pickup still has their name on it, or because school lets out early and the usual pickup is still set for 3:15. This course teaches an agent to read the household calendar and to-do list the way a careful parent would, find the problems hiding in it, and tell the owner what needs a decision. It only reads; it changes nothing.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.a.1.b.10 Review work orders or schedules to determine operations or procedures.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.5.i.4 Develop daily schedules for children or families.

## After this course, the agent can
- Find conflicts that are about people, not just times: one driver in two places, a pickup assigned to someone who is away, a child with no adult listed.
- Treat all-day and multi-day entries (trips, school closures, early dismissal) as facts that change the ordinary entries under them.
- Catch deadlines on the to-do list (forms, payments, sign-ups) that fall in the week.
- Say plainly what the calendar doesn't settle (a time to be announced, no driver listed) instead of assuming.
- Report the week with the problems first.

## Not covered
- Adding events or to-dos: HOME 210.
- Changing or cancelling anything: HOME 350.
