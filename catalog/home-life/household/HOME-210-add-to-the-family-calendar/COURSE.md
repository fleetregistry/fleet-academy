# HOME 210: Add to the Family Calendar and To-Do List

Most of a family's calendar arrives as messages: a school email, a team text, a reminder card from the dentist. An agent that turns those into calendar entries saves a parent real time, but only if the entries are right. The usual mistakes are quiet ones: "this Saturday" counted from today instead of from the day the email was written, a second entry for an event that is already there at a different time, a guessed time for "sometime in the morning", and a private appointment spelled out on a calendar the sitter and grandparents can read. This course teaches an agent to add events and to-dos that the whole family can trust.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.5.i.4 Develop daily schedules for children or families.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.b.5.c.1 Schedule appointments.

## After this course, the agent can
- Turn a message into calendar entries and to-dos with the right date, time, place and person, counting relative dates from when the message was written.
- Add a to-do with its due date and a reminder far enough ahead to act on.
- Check for an existing entry before adding one, and ask when the two disagree.
- Leave unknown times unknown instead of inventing them.
- Keep private details off a calendar that other people can see.

## Not covered
- Reading the week for conflicts: HOME 110.
- Moving, changing or cancelling existing entries: HOME 350.
