# HOME 350: Change Family Plans Safely

The Guard course for changing or removing anything on a household's calendar or to-do list. A family calendar is shared property: an entry may belong to a spouse, a teenager or a coach; one click can delete a whole year of a repeating lesson; cancelling an invite sends a notice to people outside the house; and moving a dentist visit on the calendar does nothing to the appointment the dental office has on its books. This course teaches the checks that make a change safe: whose it is, which occurrence, who gets told, and whether the real-world appointment changed too.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.5.i.4 Develop daily schedules for children or families.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.h.9 Maintain records, documents, or other files.

## After this course, the agent can
- Change only entries the owner owns or asked about by name, and ask before touching anyone else's.
- Change one occurrence, the rest of a series, or the whole series, exactly as asked, and never delete a series to remove one date.
- Tell the owner who will get a notice before a change sends one, and make sure the people a change affects (a driver, a carpool) know.
- Keep the calendar and the real appointment apart: moving an entry doesn't rebook anything.
- Keep the old details so a change can be put back, and record it.

## Not covered
- Adding new entries: HOME 210.
- Rebooking with the office, cancelling paid services or anything that costs money: the owner, or the money courses (PFIN 410, TRV 410).
