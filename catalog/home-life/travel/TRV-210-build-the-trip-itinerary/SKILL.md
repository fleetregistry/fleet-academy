# Build the Trip Itinerary

The itinerary is only as good as the confirmations behind it. Build from them, then look for what doesn't fit.

## 1. Only what's confirmed is booked
- Every item marked booked must come from a confirmation (an email, a booking page, a ticket) with a confirmation code. Copy codes, times and addresses exactly.
- Plans without a confirmation ("Grandpa said he'd reserve the airport shuttle") go in a **not booked / not confirmed** list, with who to check with. Never write them as booked, and never invent a code, time or address.

## 2. Day by day, in local time
- One section per day, in order. Each item: time, what, where, confirmation code.
- Use the local time where the item happens, and label the time zone when the trip crosses one. A flight that lands the next day goes on the next day, marked "+1".
- Add the times that matter on the ground: hotel check-in and check-out, rental pickup and return, when to leave for the airport.

## 3. Look for what doesn't fit
Walk through the trip hour by hour as the family will live it:
- **Hotel nights vs flights.** Does the first night match the night they arrive? An overnight flight that lands in the morning means the hotel may be booked for a night they spend on the plane, or they arrive hours before check-in. Does the last night match the departure?
- **Arrival vs opening hours.** Will the rental counter, shuttle or hotel desk still be open when they land? A flight that lands at 10:50 PM can't use a shuttle desk that closes at 10:30 PM.
- **Long waits.** Hours between hotel check-out and a late flight, or a very early arrival before check-in: point out, with what might help (late check-out, luggage storage).
- **Tight links.** A connection, or a pickup time that leaves no room for a delay.
For each, say what the owner might do, and by when (while cancellation is still free).

## 4. Deadlines as to-dos
List each dated deadline: free cancellation ends, online check-in opens, balance due, passport or form deadlines. Put them on the owner's to-do list or calendar if you can, with a reminder ahead.

## 5. Copies others will see
The itinerary may go to grandparents, a house-sitter or the travelers. Leave out passport and ID numbers, card numbers and account logins. Confirmation codes are fine for the travelers; for a house-sitter, dates and contact details are enough.

## Report
Lead with the problems found and anything not confirmed. Then the itinerary. Record what you created and where.
