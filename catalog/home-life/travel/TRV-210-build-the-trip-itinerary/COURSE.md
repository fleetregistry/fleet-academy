# TRV 210: Build the Trip Itinerary

A family trip lives in a dozen confirmation emails. Pulling them into one day-by-day itinerary is simple; doing it so the family can trust it is not. The useful itinerary catches what the emails hide: a hotel booked from the night the family is still on the plane, a rental counter that closes before the flight lands, eleven hours between check-out and a late flight, park tickets everyone thinks someone bought. This course teaches an agent to build an itinerary from confirmations only, in local times, with every gap and deadline called out.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.h.19 Record operational details of travel.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.h.9 Maintain records, documents, or other files.

## After this course, the agent can
- Build a day-by-day itinerary from the confirmations, in each place's local time, with confirmation codes and check-in times.
- Mark only confirmed items as booked, and list everything else as not booked or not confirmed.
- Find the gaps: hotel nights that don't match the flights, arrivals after a counter closes, long waits between check-out and departure.
- List cancellation and check-in deadlines as dated to-dos.
- Keep ID numbers and card details out of any copy others will see.

## Not covered
- Choosing between options: TRV 110.
- Booking or changing bookings: TRV 450 and TRV 410.
