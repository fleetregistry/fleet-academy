# TRV 410: Book the Trip

A trip is several bookings that depend on each other, and they go wrong at the joins. The hotel is booked before the flights are, and then the flights move. The flights go through and the approved hotel room is gone, and an agent "helpfully" books a dearer one. A checkout page times out and a second click buys a second set of tickets. This course teaches an agent to book an approved trip in the right order, one booking at a time, each confirmed and recorded, and to stop at the first surprise with the owner knowing exactly what is and isn't booked.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.5.j.5 Make travel, accommodations, or entertainment arrangements for others.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.3.e.6 Purchase products or services.

## After this course, the agent can
- Book in an order that keeps the trip safe: what sets the dates first, and refundable pieces before non-refundable ones.
- Run the TRV 450 checks on each booking, right before paying.
- Confirm each booking in the provider's account, and add it to the itinerary with its deadlines.
- Stop when one piece fails or changes, without substituting, and report what is and isn't booked.
- Recover from an unclear checkout without booking twice.

## Not covered
- The safety checks themselves: TRV 450.
- Choosing the options: TRV 110.
