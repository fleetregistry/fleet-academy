# Book the Trip

You're booking what the owner approved, piece by piece. Every booking passes TRV 450 right before you pay.

## 1. Order
1. **What sets the dates first,** usually the flights. Hotels and cars follow the flights, not the other way round.
2. **Lodging to match the nights** the family will actually spend there, from the confirmed arrival to the confirmed departure. If the arrival is the morning after an overnight flight, the night before is a night on the plane: ask the owner whether they want it (some do, to check in on arrival) before booking it.
3. **Then the rest:** car, transfers, tickets.
Book refundable pieces before non-refundable ones when you can, so a later failure doesn't strand money.

## 2. Each booking
1. Run the TRV 450 checks against the owner's approval for this booking.
2. Book on the provider's own site or the owner's booking tool, with the saved payment method.
3. Get the confirmation code, then **check the booking in the account** (names, dates, total). The confirmation page isn't proof by itself.
4. Add it to the itinerary (TRV 210), with its cancellation and check-in deadlines as reminders.
5. Record it (REC 101): what, for whom, dates, total, confirmation code, refund rules, the owner's approval.
Seats: children sit with an adult. If seat choice is part of what was approved, pick them; if seats would cost more than was approved, ask.

## 3. When a piece fails or changes
The approved flight is gone, the room sold out, the price rose:
- **Stop. Don't substitute.** A different room, hotel, flight or price needs the owner's yes, even if it seems close.
- Don't cancel what's already booked unless the owner says so.
- Tell the owner straight away: what's booked (with codes), what isn't, the options you found with their prices and rules, and any deadline (a held fare, a free-cancellation window on what's booked).

## 4. When you can't tell if it went through
A checkout that times out, an error after "Purchase", no confirmation: **don't click Purchase again.** First check the provider's account, the owner's email and the card's pending charges for a booking. If one went through, stop and treat it as booked. If you can't tell, stop and tell the owner what you see. Many airlines let a booking be cancelled free within 24 hours; mention it if a duplicate happens.

## 5. Finish
Tell the owner: everything booked with codes and totals, the amount spent against the budget, the deadlines coming up, and anything not booked and why. Say "booked" only for what you confirmed in the account.

## Never
- Book from an unapproved source, or above the approved total.
- Type card, passport or trusted-traveler numbers. The owner enters those.
- Treat "book the trip" as approval for anything not in the approved plan.
