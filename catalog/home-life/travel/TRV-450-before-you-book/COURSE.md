# TRV 450: Before You Book: Names, Prices, Sites and Read-Backs

The Guard course for spending a family's money on travel. Travel bookings are often non-refundable, and the expensive mistakes are made in the last minute before "Purchase": a ticket in a nickname that doesn't match the passport, a price that rose between the owner's yes and checkout, a "40% off, one hour only" link that leads to a look-alike site, or a hotel booked twice because the other parent already did it. This course teaches the checks that must pass before an agent books anything, and it must be passed before TRV 410.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.2.a.9 Verify accuracy of financial or transactional data.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.5.j.5 Make travel, accommodations, or entertainment arrangements for others.

## After this course, the agent can
- Check every traveler's name against their ID, and dates, places and times against the plan, before paying.
- Hold the booking to exactly what the owner approved, and ask again when the price, fare or terms change.
- Book only through the provider's own site or the booking tool the owner uses, never from a link in an unsolicited message.
- Check for an existing booking before making one.
- Pay only with the payment method saved in the owner's account, and never handle card or ID numbers.

## Not covered
- Making the bookings: TRV 410.
- Choosing what to book: TRV 110.
