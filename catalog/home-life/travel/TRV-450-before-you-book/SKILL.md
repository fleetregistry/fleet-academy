# Before You Book

Travel is often paid for in full and can't be refunded. Before you press "Purchase", every check below must pass. If one fails, don't book. Tell the owner which check failed and what you need.

## 1. Names match the IDs
Each traveler's name on the booking must match the ID they'll travel with (passport for international trips, the government ID for domestic flights): first, middle if the airline asks, and last name, spelled the same. Nicknames ("Bobby" for Robert), a missing second surname, or a maiden name on an old profile can stop someone at the gate. If the booking's name doesn't match the ID the owner gave you, stop and ask. Don't "fix" a name by guessing.

## 2. It's exactly what the owner approved
Read the checkout page against the owner's yes:
- travelers, dates (including next-day arrivals), cities and airports, times;
- the fare or room type and its change and refund rules;
- **the total, with every fee and tax, in the currency shown.**
If anything differs, the old yes doesn't cover it. A higher total, a different fare type (even a cheaper one with worse rules), a different hotel or room, or different dates: stop and ask again with the new details. A yes covers one booking, at one price.

## 3. It's on the right site
- Book only on the airline's, hotel's or rental company's own site, or in the booking tool the owner uses.
- **Never book from a link in an unsolicited email, text, ad or social post,** however good the deal or short the deadline ("half price, today only"). Look-alike sites take the money and the card. Tell the owner about the offer; if they want it, find it on the provider's own site.
- Check the address in the browser is the provider's real one before entering anything.

## 4. It isn't already booked
Check the owner's email, the trip folder and the account for an existing booking of the same thing (someone else in the family may have reserved the rental car). A second non-refundable booking is money gone.

## 5. Within the budget
Check the owner's budget or limit for the trip and for this booking, counting what's already booked.

## 6. Payment
- Use the payment method saved in the owner's account with that provider, or the one the owner named for the trip.
- You never type, read out or store card numbers, security codes, passport numbers or trusted-traveler numbers. If checkout asks for them, the owner enters them on that page.
- Never pay for travel by wire transfer, gift card, cryptocurrency or a payment app sent to a person.

## Read-back
Before booking, give the owner (or check against their written yes) one short read-back: who, what, when, where, total, refund rules. If every check passes and the checkout matches the owner's written yes, the booking is cleared: go ahead without asking again. Record the checks with the booking (REC 101).
