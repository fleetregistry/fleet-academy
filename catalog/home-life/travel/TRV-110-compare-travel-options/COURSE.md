# TRV 110: Compare Travel Options

The cheapest fare on the screen is often not the cheapest trip. For a family, bag fees, the fee to sit together, a hotel's nightly resort fee and parking turn a "deal" into the dearer option. A short connection on two separate tickets can strand everyone with no rebooking. And the best price is worthless if a child's passport won't be accepted at the border. This course teaches an agent to compare trips the way the family will actually take them: total cost for the whole party, real times, real risks, and documents, before anything is booked.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.b.1.a.6 Estimate costs of goods or services.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.3.c.12 Provide transportation information to passengers or customers.

## After this course, the agent can
- Price each option for the whole party, with every fee the family will really pay.
- Read times correctly (local times, next-day arrivals) and flag tight or risky connections, especially on separate tickets.
- Compare change and refund rules.
- Check travel documents against the rules the destination states, before any booking.
- Present a few options with a recommendation tied to what the owner said matters, and say what's unknown.

## Not covered
- Building the itinerary from confirmations: TRV 210.
- Booking anything: TRV 450 and TRV 410.
- Immigration or visa advice: the destination's government and, if needed, a qualified adviser.
