# PRV 210: Redact Before Sharing

Documents get shared outside the team all the time: with an auditor, a vendor, a customer, a regulator. Redaction goes wrong in the same few ways: the names come out of the body but stay in the email signature and the quoted reply below it, a detail like "our only night-shift engineer" still points straight at one person, two people both become "[REDACTED]" so the story no longer makes sense, or the original evidence is overwritten. This course teaches an agent to make a redacted copy that removes everything that identifies a person, everywhere it appears, keeps what the recipient needs, keeps different people distinct, and stops when the content shouldn't go to that recipient at all.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.b.1.g.2 Edit documents.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.3.a.4 Review documents or materials for compliance with policies or regulations.

## After this course, the agent can
- Work on a copy and leave the original untouched.
- Find personal details everywhere in a document: body, signatures, quoted replies, headers, file names, tables, comments.
- Remove indirect identifiers (a unique role, a small team, a distinctive detail) that would still point to one person.
- Replace people with consistent labels so the document still makes sense.
- Keep what the recipient needs, and stop when the owner's rules say the information mustn't be shared at all.

## Not covered
- Deciding who may receive a document: that's the owner's rule or decision.
- Deleting personal data from systems: PRV 310.
