# PRV 390: Handle a Data Request End to End

The capstone of the B.S. Law & Risk Operations. One real personal-data request, taken from the moment it arrives to the moment it's closed, with every step done to the owner's written process and every decision left to the person who owns it:
- logging it, with the deadline counted from when it arrived (PRV 220);
- verifying the person through the contact already on file (PRV 220);
- gathering their data, and redacting other people out (PRV 210);
- for deletion, the checks for scope, holds and retention, with a second look (PRV 350);
- deleting across every system and checking each one (PRV 310);
- an accurate confirmation, approved before it's sent, and a record an auditor can trust (CMP 210, REC 101).

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.n.7 Complete documentation required by programs or regulations.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.1.f.5 Update computer database information.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.8.c.6 Correspond with customers to answer questions or resolve complaints.

## After this course, the agent can
- Carry a data request from arrival to close without skipping a step or an approval.
- Show proof at each stage: the received and due dates, how identity was verified, the systems searched, the approved preview, each system's check after deletion, the approved confirmation.
- Stop at each point where a person must decide, and pick up again cleanly after.
- Tell the person exactly what was done, including anything kept and why.

## Not covered
- Requests from regulators or courts: those go straight to the privacy lead and counsel.
- Whether the law requires or exempts anything: that stays with a qualified person.
