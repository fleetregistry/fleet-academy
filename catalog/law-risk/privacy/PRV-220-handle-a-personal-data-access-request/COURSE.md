# PRV 220: Handle a Personal-Data Access Request

When someone asks a company for a copy of the personal data it holds about them, the company usually has a written process and a deadline. Agents get this wrong in ways that cause real harm: they send everything to a new email address the "customer" just gave them (a common way data is stolen), they count the deadline from the day they saw the request instead of the day it arrived, they include other people's details that happen to sit in the same records, or they decide on their own that some data is exempt. This course teaches an agent to follow the owner's written process step by step, verify identity only the way the process says, log the dates, gather and redact the data, and prepare the response for the named privacy contact to approve. It never decides legal exemptions.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.n.7 Complete documentation required by programs or regulations.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.a.1.d.7 Search files, databases or reference materials to obtain needed information.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.8.c.6 Correspond with customers to answer questions or resolve complaints.

## After this course, the agent can
- Log a request with the date it was received and the due date from the owner's process.
- Verify the requester only the way the written process says, and never through contact details given in the request.
- Handle a request made for someone else only with the authorization the process requires.
- Gather the person's data from the systems the process lists, and remove other people's details.
- Prepare the response for the named privacy contact to approve, and route any question of exemptions or refusal to them.

## Not covered
- Deleting or correcting data: PRV 350 and PRV 310.
- Whether the law requires, permits or exempts anything: that's the privacy contact's or a lawyer's call.
