# PRV 350: Before You Delete Personal Data: Scope, Holds and a Second Look

This is the Guard course for deleting or changing personal data. A deletion can't be taken back, and the ways it goes wrong are well known: deleting a different person who shares the name, deleting records the company is required to keep or has been told to preserve for a dispute, deleting before the request was verified or approved, and deleting more than was approved because new records appeared since the preview. This course teaches the checks that stop all of them, and it must be passed before an agent deletes or corrects personal data.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.2.a.1 Verify accuracy of records.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.3.a.4 Review documents or materials for compliance with policies or regulations.

## After this course, the agent can
- Confirm the request was verified and approved in writing by the named person under the owner's process.
- Match records by unique identifiers, not names, and keep a same-named person's records out of scope.
- Check the legal hold list and the retention schedule, and keep what they require without deciding exceptions itself.
- Preview the exact records, get a person's second look, and stop if the count changes.
- Record what was done without copying the deleted data into the record.

## Not covered
- Carrying out the deletion across systems: PRV 310.
- Whether a hold or retention rule applies in law: that's the privacy lead's or a lawyer's call.
