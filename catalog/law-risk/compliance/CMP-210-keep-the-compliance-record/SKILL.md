# Keep the Compliance Record

The record is evidence. Every entry must be something you'd be comfortable having an auditor test.

## Every entry says
1. **What was done:** the control or task, and its scope ("Q3 access review, finance systems").
2. **Who did it.**
3. **When it happened:** the real date it was done, not the due date, and not today's date if it was done earlier.
4. **When it was recorded:** today, if different.
5. **Evidence:** a link to or name of the thing that proves it (the export, the signed form, the ticket). If there's no evidence, say "no evidence attached."
6. **Due date and status:** on time, late (with how late), partial, or not done.

## Only what the evidence shows
- If the evidence covers 10 of 12 people, the entry says 10 of 12, and names the 2 not shown. Never record "all complete" because a manager said so.
- A person's statement ("they've all done it") can be recorded as what it is: "Manager states all 12 completed; system export shows 10." It isn't evidence of completion.
- Missing is a status, not a blank. Keep gaps visible.

## Late is late
If something was done after its due date, record the actual date and mark it late. Don't record it under the due date, the quarter-end, or "on or before."

## Corrections are new entries
Never edit or delete a past entry, even to fix a typo in a date. Add a new entry: "Correction to entry 14: the review date was September 21, not September 12. Corrected by [you] on [date], source [evidence]." The original stays, so anyone can see what changed and why.

## Requests to make it say something untrue
If anyone asks you to backdate an entry, mark incomplete work complete, drop a gap, or rewrite an old entry, even "just this once," even "the auditors only care that it's done":
- Don't do it.
- Say plainly why: the record would then be false, and a false compliance record is worse than a late one.
- Record the true facts, and tell your owner (or the compliance lead your rules name) about the request.

## Before you save
Read the entry back against the evidence: names, counts, dates. If anything doesn't match, fix the entry before saving, or stop and ask.

Record your own action in your log as well (REC 101).
