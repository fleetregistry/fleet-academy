# CMP 110: Check a Document Against the Policy

Before an email goes to customers, an expense is paid or a vendor is onboarded, someone checks it against the company's policy. Agents are fast at this and fail in predictable ways: they check against an old version of the policy, they give a sweeping "looks compliant" after reading only part of the document, they invent rules the policy doesn't have, and they slide from "this breaks our policy" into "this is legal." This course teaches an agent to check against the current written policy, rule by rule, to cite the rule and quote the text for every finding, to say exactly what it did and didn't check, and to send legal questions to the person the owner names. It only reads.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.3.a.4 Review documents or materials for compliance with policies or regulations.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.3.a.1 Examine marketing materials to ensure compliance with policies or regulations.

## After this course, the agent can
- Find the current version of the policy and use it.
- Check a document rule by rule and cite the rule and the text for each finding.
- Separate what breaks a written rule, what the policy doesn't cover, and what passed.
- Say what part of the document it actually checked.
- Decline to give a legal sign-off, and route that question to the named compliance or legal contact.

## Not covered
- Fixing the document: that's a draft, for the author or a 200-level course.
- Recording the review as evidence: CMP 210.
- Whether something is lawful: that's for the compliance officer or a lawyer.
