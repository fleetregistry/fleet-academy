# CTR 210: Draft a Notice for the Owner to Send

A contract notice (not renewing, ending, changing an address) only works if it follows the contract's notice clause: the right party, the right address, the right method, on time. A friendly email to the account rep usually counts for nothing, and an agent that writes one has given the owner false comfort. This course teaches an agent to read the notice clause first, draft a notice that meets it, using the exact legal names, saying only what the owner decided, and to hand it to the owner to sign and send. It flags at once when the deadline can't be met by the required method. It never sends a notice itself.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.n.9 Prepare legal documents.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.1.a.4 Prepare business correspondence.

## After this course, the agent can
- Find the contract's notice clause and list its requirements: method, address, attention line, copies, and when notice takes effect.
- Draft a notice to the exact legal party at the required address, naming the agreement and the effective date.
- Tell the owner when the method they asked for (an email, a message to a rep) won't count as notice under the clause.
- Flag, before drafting, when the required method can't reach the other side in time.
- Hand the draft to the owner to sign and send, with a short checklist, and never send it itself.

## Not covered
- Working out the deadline: CTR 120.
- Deciding whether to end or renew: that's the owner's decision.
- Notices that make legal claims or threats: those go to a lawyer.
