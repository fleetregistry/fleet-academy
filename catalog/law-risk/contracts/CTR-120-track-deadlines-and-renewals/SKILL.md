# Track Deadlines and Renewals

The renewal date is rarely the deadline. The deadline is the **last day the owner can act**, and it's often 30 to 120 days earlier.

## For each contract, find four things
1. **End of the current term.** Start date plus term length, or the date stated. If it renewed before, the current term is the latest renewal.
2. **What happens at the end.** Renews automatically (for how long), ends, or rolls month to month.
3. **The notice rule.** How many days, before what date, and whether notice must be *given* (sent) or *received* by then. Note the required method (written, courier, a set address) for whoever drafts the notice.
4. **Amendments.** Read every amendment, and use the latest one that changes the term, the renewal or the notice period. Say which document each number came from.

## Count back, and show it
- Last day = end of term minus the notice period, counted in calendar days unless the contract says business days.
- Write the sum: "Term ends March 31, 2027. 90 days' notice. Last day to give notice: December 31, 2026."
- If the wording leaves the exact day unclear, use the earlier possible day and say why.
- If notice must be **received** by the date, say so, and note that the owner has to send it early enough to arrive.

## Sort by the day action is due
List contracts in order of the last notice day, not the end date. A contract that ends later can need action sooner. Put the days left next to each one ("12 days from today").

## When a window has closed
Say it plainly and first: "The last day to give notice was October 31, 2026. Under clause 3, the contract renews on its own for another 12 months." Don't put it on a list for later as if it can still be met, and don't suggest the notice will still work if sent now. Whether anything can be done (asking the other side, a lawyer's view) is the owner's decision; say that.

## When you can't compute it
If the date depends on something you don't have (a "go-live date," a start date that isn't recorded, a missing amendment), **don't assume one**. Say which fact is missing, where it might be found, and give the rule so the date can be filled in once it's known.

## Report
Lead with the most urgent action and its date. Then one line per contract: name, last notice day, how you got it, days left, and the notice method. Then any closed windows and anything you couldn't compute.

## Don't
- Send notices, or create or change calendar entries. This course only reads.
- Give a view on whether a deadline is binding or can be challenged. That's for a lawyer.
