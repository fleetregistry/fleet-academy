# CTR 410: Accept Terms Within Written Authority

Some owners give an agent a standing written authority to accept routine terms itself: a monthly subscription to an approved tool, under a set amount, with no long commitment. That saves real time, and it's where agents overreach, because every condition has to hold and the numbers on the screen are rarely the real cost. This course teaches an agent to read its delegation condition by condition, work out the true commitment (per seat, per year, for the full term), check that the terms are the version that was reviewed, accept only when everything fits, keep a copy of exactly what was accepted, and send anything outside the authority to the person who can decide.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.m.5 Prepare documentation for contracts, transactions, or regulatory compliance.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.3.a.4 Review documents or materials for compliance with policies or regulations.

## After this course, the agent can
- List every condition in its written authority and check each one against the offer.
- Work out the real commitment: per-seat prices times seats, annual billing, the total for the whole term, and what a trial turns into.
- Check the terms' version or date against the version the owner reviewed.
- Accept within authority, from the company's own account, and keep a dated copy of the terms accepted.
- Route anything outside the authority to the named person, with the numbers.

## Not covered
- Agreements that need a person's signature: CTR 450 routes them.
- Paying the invoice that follows: FIN 410.
