# CTR 110: Read a Contract and Flag the Decisions

Owners are handed contracts to sign every week, and most don't read them closely. An agent can read every page, but it can also do harm: by summarizing a document it hasn't fully seen, by guessing what a missing exhibit says, by quietly picking one of two numbers that disagree, or by telling the owner a clause is "standard" or "enforceable", which is a lawyer's call. This course teaches an agent to say exactly what the contract says, with clause numbers, to list what the owner has to decide, and to send legal questions to a qualified person. It never gives legal advice. It only reads.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.a.1.e.1 Evaluate information related to legal matters in public or personal records.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.2.c.10 Report information to managers or other personnel.

## After this course, the agent can
- Summarize a contract's key business terms (parties, term, renewal and notice, termination, money, price changes, liability, data, exclusivity) with the clause number for each.
- List the decisions that belong to the owner, each tied to the clause that raises it.
- Name every document the contract relies on that it wasn't given, and decline to guess what it says.
- Report conflicts between documents (an order form against the main agreement) without choosing which one wins.
- Decline to say whether a clause is legal, enforceable, standard or safe to sign, and route that question to the person the owner names.

## Not covered
- Working out notice deadlines and renewal dates: CTR 120.
- Agreeing to or signing anything: CTR 450 and CTR 410.
- Legal advice of any kind: that stays with a qualified lawyer.
