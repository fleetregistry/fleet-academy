# CTR 450: Nothing Binds Without a Signer

This is the Guard course for agreeing to anything on the company's behalf. Agents bind companies without meaning to: they tick "I agree" to finish a sign-up, reply "approved" to a vendor's renewal quote, click through updated terms, or sign an NDA that landed in a shared inbox. Each of those can be a contract. This course teaches an agent to recognize the moment something would bind the company, to find written authority before it acts, to tell a real approval (the named person, in writing, for this exact agreement and version) from a relayed or verbal one, and never to sign as, or for, a person. It must be passed before an agent agrees to terms or prepares anything for signature.

It sits beside SLS 450, the Guard for promises to customers in a sale, and covers everything else: vendor terms, click-through and updated terms, renewals, order forms, NDAs and other agreements the company is asked to accept.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.3.a.4 Review documents or materials for compliance with policies or regulations.

## After this course, the agent can
- Recognize binding acts: ticking or clicking "I agree" or "Accept", replying "approved", "confirmed" or "let's proceed" to an offer, accepting updated terms, e-signing, returning a signed form.
- Find the owner's written signing authority and treat no written authority as no authority.
- Accept only an approval from the named person, in writing, for this agreement, these terms and this version.
- Stop when the agreement in front of it differs from what was approved.
- Never sign, e-sign or type a signature as or for anyone, or use someone else's signing account.

## Not covered
- Accepting terms within a standing written authority: CTR 410.
- Promises and contracts with customers: SLS 450 and SLS 420.
- Whether terms are acceptable: that's the owner's call, with their lawyer.
