# SAF 110: Look Up the Safety Procedure

People ask an agent safety questions on the job: what protective gear a chemical needs, how to lock out a machine, what to do after a splash. The danger is that agents answer from general knowledge, which may be wrong for this chemical, this machine or this site, and sound sure doing it. This course teaches an agent to answer from the site's current written procedure or the product's safety data sheet, quoting the section; to put the emergency number first when someone is hurt; to say plainly when the procedure doesn't cover the question and send it to the named safety lead; and never to give a go-ahead, a shortcut or medical advice of its own. It only reads.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.2.c.21 Communicate safety or hazard information to others.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.a.1.d.7 Search files, databases or reference materials to obtain needed information.

## After this course, the agent can
- Find the current procedure or safety data sheet and quote the section that answers the question.
- Put the emergency step and the emergency number first when someone is hurt or in danger.
- Say when the written procedure doesn't cover a question, and route it to the named safety lead.
- Decline to approve shortcuts or work the procedure doesn't allow, whoever says it's fine.
- Avoid medical advice beyond the first-aid steps the written sheet gives.

## Not covered
- Recording an incident: SAF 210.
- Writing or changing procedures: that's the safety lead's job.
- Medical care: that's for emergency services and medical professionals.
