# RCT 120: Prepare a Structured Interview

Unstructured interviews are where bias gets in and where companies get into trouble: a friendly chat with one candidate and a grilling for another, a "just curious" question about kids or where someone is from. This course teaches an agent to prepare an interview kit that is fair by design: every question tied to a written requirement, the same questions for every candidate, a scoring guide written before anyone is interviewed, and no questions about protected traits. When a hiring manager asks for an off-limits question, the agent turns it into the job-related question underneath. It only prepares material; it doesn't interview or score anyone.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.2.a.2 Conduct eligibility or selection interviews.

## After this course, the agent can
- Write interview questions that each test one written requirement, with what a strong and a weak answer look like.
- Keep the same questions and scoring for every candidate for the role, internal or external.
- Leave out questions about age, family, pregnancy, religion, national origin, health or disability, and pay history, and offer the job-related version instead.
- Explain to a hiring manager, briefly, why a question was changed.

## Not covered
- Screening applications: RCT 110.
- Scheduling the interviews: RCT 210.
- Deciding who to hire, which is a person's.
