# Schedule an Interview Loop

## Know the panel and the length
From the hiring plan, list who **must** be on each interview (often the hiring manager) and how long each one is. A slot works only if it is long enough and **every required person** is free for all of it. Partial overlaps don't count.

If no slot works for a required interviewer, don't swap in someone else or drop them. Ask the candidate for more times, or ask the owner what to do.

## Time zones
- Use the time zone the candidate gave you, not where you think they are. If they didn't give one, ask.
- Convert carefully, checking daylight saving time for that date in both places.
- Offer times **in the candidate's time zone, named** ("Wednesday, November 11, 2:00 to 2:45 pm Lisbon time"). Put the interviewers' time beside it in the internal invite.
- Offer two or three options, all inside the hours the candidate said they're free.

## Two audiences, two invites
- **The candidate's invite:** date, time in their zone, length, how to join or where to go, who they'll meet (names and roles), and a contact for questions. Nothing else.
- **The interviewers' invite:** the same time, plus the résumé, the questions, the scorecard and any notes.
Never put internal notes, feedback, scores, other candidates' names, or pay discussion where the candidate can see it, including the invite description, attachments and the guest list.

## Accommodations
If a candidate asks for an accommodation (more time, a quiet room, captions, an accessible entrance), arrange it and confirm it with them. Tell interviewers only what they need to *do* ("the interview is 90 minutes", "captions are on"). Never pass on the reason, a diagnosis, or the candidate's words about their health, to interviewers or to the hiring manager. Send the request itself to HR, as the company's process says.

## Don't speak for the hiring team
Don't promise outcomes, timelines or pay. Questions about pay or the decision go to the recruiter or the owner.

## Report
The times offered (both zones), who is booked, what the candidate was sent, and anything you couldn't arrange. Record it (REC 101).
