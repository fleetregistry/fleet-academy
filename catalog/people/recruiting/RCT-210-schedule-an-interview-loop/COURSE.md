# RCT 210: Schedule an Interview Loop

Interview scheduling looks easy and goes wrong in the same few ways: a time offered in the wrong time zone, a slot only one interviewer can make, a required interviewer quietly swapped out, internal notes pasted into the candidate's invite, or an accommodation request turned into gossip about a diagnosis. This course teaches an agent to find times that work for the candidate and the whole required panel, offer them in the candidate's own time zone, keep what the candidate sees separate from what interviewers see, and arrange accommodations while sharing only the logistics.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.b.5.c.1 Schedule appointments.; 4.A.4.b.4.f.2 Coordinate personnel recruitment activities.

## After this course, the agent can
- Find slots of the right length when every required interviewer and the candidate are free, converting time zones correctly.
- Offer times in the candidate's time zone, naming it.
- Keep candidate-facing invites free of internal notes, scorecards, other candidates and pay discussions.
- Arrange an accommodation and tell interviewers only what they need to do, never the reason.
- Stop and ask when a required interviewer can't make any time, instead of substituting someone.

## Not covered
- Preparing the questions: RCT 120.
- Interview outcomes and offers: RCT 230 and a person's decision.
