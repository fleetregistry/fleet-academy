# Build a Training Plan

## Find the gaps
1. List the role's **written** skill requirements and the **required trainings** (with deadlines) from policy.
2. Mark what the person **already has**, from their records or what their manager or they have said. Leave those out.
3. What's left is the plan. Each item names the gap or requirement it closes.

If you don't know where the person stands on a requirement, don't assume a gap: plan a short check first (a conversation, a skills check, a sample task), or ask.

## Only real gaps, never assumptions about the person
Base the plan on the role's requirements and evidence of the person's work. Never on age, health, disability, pregnancy, family, national origin, or any other personal trait, and never on a guess like "older people struggle with computers". If a manager offers one, ask for the specific skill, against the role's requirements, that is falling short, and an example.

## Required first
Put required trainings first, scheduled to finish before their deadlines.

## Fit the time budget
- Use the time the owner set ("3 hours a week for 4 weeks" = 12 hours).
- Give each item its time, and show the total.
- If the required items alone don't fit before their deadlines, say so plainly and give the choices (more time, a later deadline from whoever sets it, a shorter option). Don't quietly squeeze or drop a required item.
- If development items don't fit, say which ones wait.

## Cheapest good option, and money is a person's call
Prefer internal or free options that close the gap. A paid course, certificate, conference or tool can go in the plan as a **proposal**, with its cost, for the budget owner to approve. Don't enroll anyone in anything paid.

## Order and checks
Put items in a sensible order (foundations first). Give each one a way to check it was learned: a passed assessment, a task done on the job, feedback from a named person.

## Report
The plan as a short table: item, the gap it closes, time, by when, how it's checked, cost if any. The total time against the budget, anything that didn't fit, and anything waiting for approval.
