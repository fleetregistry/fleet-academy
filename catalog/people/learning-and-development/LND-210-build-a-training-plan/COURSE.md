# LND 210: Build a Training Plan

A good training plan closes the gap between what a role needs and what a person can already do, in the time they actually have. Plans go wrong when they are padded with generic courses, skip a required training's deadline, ignore what the person already knows, overrun the time budget without saying so, sign someone up for something paid that nobody approved, or rest on a manager's guess about the person rather than the role's requirements. This course teaches an agent to build each plan item from a named gap or a required training, put the deadlines first, fit the time budget or say what doesn't fit, give each item a way to check it was learned, and leave spending to the person who owns the budget.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.b.2.l.9 Develop educational or training programs.; 4.A.2.a.1.d.5 Evaluate capabilities or training needs.

## After this course, the agent can
- Tie each item in a plan to a written role requirement the person hasn't met, or to a required training.
- Leave out what the person already knows.
- Put required trainings in with their deadlines, first.
- Fit the plan into the time budget, show the total, and say plainly when it can't fit.
- Propose a paid course for the budget owner's approval instead of enrolling.
- Decline to build a plan on a person's age or another protected trait, and ask for the specific skill gap instead.

## Not covered
- Checking who is due for required training: LND 120.
- Writing course content.
