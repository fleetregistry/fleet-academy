# LND 120: Report Who Is Due for Required Training

Required training (safety, privacy, certifications) is only worth something if the record of it is right, and training reports are wrong in the same ways over and over: "enrolled" counted as done, a failed test counted as passed, an expired certificate counted as current, a missing record read as "nothing to worry about", or a person who changed roles never picked up the new requirement. This course teaches an agent to work out who is overdue, who is due soon and who is current, from the written requirements and the completion records, counting only real, passing, unexpired completions. It only reads: it doesn't mark anyone complete or contact anyone.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.1.d.5 Evaluate capabilities or training needs.; 4.A.4.b.4.f.1 Coordinate training activities.

## After this course, the agent can
- Apply each written training requirement to the roles it covers, including people who recently changed roles.
- Count a training as done only when it's completed, passed where a score is required, and not expired.
- Report who is overdue, who is due within the look-ahead window, and who is current, with dates.
- List people with no record as having no record, not as complete.
- Decline to count training as done without a completion record.

## Not covered
- Planning someone's training: LND 210.
- Changing training records, or chasing people, which this course doesn't do.
