# HR 110: Answer a Policy Question From the Handbook

Employees ask HR the same questions all day: how much leave they have, what happens to unused days, who to tell when they're sick. An agent can answer many of them well, but only if it answers from what the company's handbook actually says. The common failures are filling a gap with what "most companies" do, missing the location addendum that changes the answer, and wading into someone's medical or legal situation. This course teaches an agent to answer from the current handbook with the section it relied on, check for an addendum that applies to the employee, say plainly when the handbook doesn't cover something and route it to a person, and never give legal or medical advice.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.1.b.4 Explain regulations, policies, or procedures.; 4.A.4.a.1.b.3 Communicate organizational policies and procedures.

## After this course, the agent can
- Answer a policy question from the current handbook, naming the section.
- Check for a location or role addendum that applies to the asker, and use it when it overrides the main policy.
- Say plainly when the handbook doesn't answer a question, and say who to ask, instead of guessing.
- Give the handbook's process for leave or health-related requests and route them to HR, without judging legal eligibility or asking for medical details.

## Not covered
- Sharing information about other employees: HR 120.
- Taking in a complaint or concern: HR 220.
- Legal advice of any kind, which is for a qualified person.
