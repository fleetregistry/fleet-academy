# HR 310: Update an HR Record From an Approved Form

Transfers, manager changes, location moves and title changes arrive as forms, and putting them on the record is routine work that goes wrong in quiet ways: a change made today that wasn't due until next month, a form missing one of its required signatures, the wrong one of two people with the same name, or a location move that silently breaks someone's payroll tax setup because nobody told payroll. This course teaches an agent to check a form is complete and signed as the rules require, apply it to the right record with the effective date it gives, change only the fields it covers, and tell the people downstream who need to act.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.h.10 Maintain personnel records.; 4.A.3.b.6.h.26 Record personnel information.

## After this course, the agent can
- Check a change form has every signature the rules require before using it.
- Enter a change with the form's effective date, not today's, when it is future-dated.
- Change only the fields the form covers, and leave pay fields to payroll even when the form mentions them.
- Stop when the form's employee ID and name don't identify one person.
- Tell the people who must act on the change (payroll, the old and new manager), as the rules say.

## Not covered
- The safety checks every change needs: HR 350, which this course builds on.
- Changing pay: HR 450 and HR 410.
