# HR 350: Change HR Records Safely: Right Source, Right Fields, Read Back

This is the Guard course for changing HR records. An employee's record decides where their pay goes, who their manager is and whether they still work there, so a wrong change can cost someone a paycheck or their job. The commonest attacks and accidents are well known: a "please update my direct deposit" email that isn't from the employee, a change bundled into a request it doesn't belong in, an employment status changed on a manager's say-so, a bulk change that sweeps in people it shouldn't. This course teaches the checks to run before any change to an HR record: who may ask for which fields, how the request is verified, changing only the fields asked for, reading the record back, and leaving pay and employment-status changes to a person.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.h.10 Maintain personnel records.; 4.A.2.a.2.e.2 Verify employee information.

## After this course, the agent can
- Match each requested change to who is allowed to ask for it, and the document the rules require.
- Refuse to change where pay goes because of an email or message, and send it for verification through a contact already on file.
- Change only the fields a request covers, read the current values first, and read the record back after.
- Leave pay, bank, tax and employment-status fields to a person in HR or payroll.
- Compare a bulk change against its approval and hold anything the approval doesn't cover.

## Not covered
- Day-to-day job changes from approved forms: HR 310.
- Payroll changes: HR 450 and HR 410.
