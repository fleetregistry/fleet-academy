# Build an Onboarding Checklist

## Start from the template
Use the company's onboarding template for this **role, location and employment type** (full time, part time, contractor). Include the role-specific and location-specific items. Don't add policies or steps the company doesn't have; if the template seems to be missing something important, flag it rather than inventing it.

## Every item: what, who, by when
- **Owner:** a named person or team ("IT: Priya Nair", "the hiring manager, Leo Brandt"), never "someone" or "TBD".
- **Due date:** a real date. Work **backward** from the start date using each item's lead time. If a laptop takes 5 business days to arrive, it must be ordered at least 5 business days before day one. Count business days, not calendar days, when the lead time is in business days.
- Group items: before day one, day one, first week, first 30 days.

## Check the timeline now
Compare today's date with each "before day one" item's latest start. If anything can't be ready in time, say so **now**, at the top, with options (a loaner laptop, a later start, a temporary setup). The start date is the owner's and the hiring manager's call: don't change it yourself.

## Paperwork and documents
List identity, work-eligibility and tax paperwork with its deadline and the HR person who checks it. The new hire submits documents and ID numbers through the company's secure HR system. Never ask for passport scans, ID numbers, bank details or tax forms by email or chat, and never put them in the checklist (SEC 101).

## Access: only what the role gets
Request exactly the systems the role template lists. If someone asks for more (admin rights, production access, another team's tools), don't request it; route it for the approval the template names.

## Required training
Include the required trainings with their deadlines (for example "data privacy, within 30 days").

## Benefits
Include the enrollment deadline and where to enroll. Don't recommend plans; point to the plan documents and the benefits contact.

## Report
Any timing problems first, then the checklist. Record that you built it (REC 101).
