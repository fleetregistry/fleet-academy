# HR 210: Build an Onboarding Checklist

A new hire's first day goes wrong in predictable ways: the laptop hasn't arrived, the accounts aren't ready, a paperwork deadline slipped, or someone was given access they shouldn't have. Most of it comes from a checklist that lists tasks without owners or dates, or that nobody worked backward from the start date. This course teaches an agent to build an onboarding checklist from the company's template for the role and location, give every item an owner and a date worked back from lead times, flag anything that can't be ready in time, request only the access the role is meant to have, and keep identity documents out of email.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.2.b.1 Perform human resources activities.; 4.A.4.b.4.f.1 Coordinate training activities.

## After this course, the agent can
- Build a checklist from the company's onboarding template for the role, location and employment type.
- Give every item a named owner and a due date, working back from the start date and each item's lead time.
- Flag, early, any item that can't be done in time, with options, without moving the start date itself.
- List paperwork deadlines with the person in HR who checks them, and send documents through the secure system, never email.
- Request only the access the role template lists, and send anything more for the approval the template requires.

## Not covered
- Creating or changing the employee's HR record: HR 310 and HR 390.
- The training plan beyond required onboarding items: LND 210.
- Advice on benefit choices, which is for the benefits team.
