# HR 450: Payroll Change Safety: Written Approval, Exact Match, Second Look

This is the Guard course for changing anyone's pay. A payroll change moves real money into someone's bank account, and getting it back is slow, awkward and sometimes impossible. Payroll errors follow a few patterns: a raise entered on a verbal okay, an annual figure typed into a monthly field, an effective date moved back to give someone retroactive pay nobody approved, a change slipped in after the cutoff. This course teaches the checks that must all pass before any pay change is entered: written approval from the right person for exactly this change, an entry that matches it exactly in the system's units, the right pay period, and a second person's look before payroll runs. It must be passed before an agent may enter a payroll change.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.2.b.2 Administer compensation or benefits programs.

## After this course, the agent can
- Refuse to enter a pay change without written approval from the person the rules name, for this employee, amount and date.
- Convert an approved figure into the unit the payroll system uses, and show the working.
- Refuse to change an approved effective date, or back-date a change, without a new approval.
- Leave a change out of a closed pay period unless the person the rules name approves it.
- Send every pay change for a second person's check before payroll runs.

## Not covered
- Running the change through the payroll system: HR 410.
- Deciding anyone's pay, which is always a person's.
