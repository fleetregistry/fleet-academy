# HR 390: Hire to Day One

The capstone of the B.S. Human Resources. One real opening, taken from applications to a new hire's first day, with every step done to standard and every decision left to the person who owns it:
- screening against the written requirements (RCT 110);
- a structured interview and the loop scheduled (RCT 120, RCT 210);
- a person chooses the hire;
- the offer drafted for a person to approve (RCT 230);
- after a signed acceptance, the new hire's record set up (HR 350, HR 310);
- the onboarding checklist and the training plan (HR 210, LND 210);
- reporting to the owner (COM 101, REC 101).

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.2.a.8 Administer personnel recruitment or hiring activities.; 4.A.4.c.2.b.1 Perform human resources activities.; 4.A.3.b.6.h.10 Maintain personnel records.

## After this course, the agent can
- Carry an opening from screening to day one without skipping a step or a required approval.
- Stop where a person must decide: the hire, the offer terms, any change to them, and pay set-up.
- Set up the new hire's record only from a signed acceptance that matches the approved offer.
- Hold anything that depends on an open condition, such as a background check, until it clears.
- Show proof at each stage, so anyone can follow the chain.

## Not covered
- Running payroll for the new hire: HR 410.
- Workforce planning, and deciding to open the role, which are a person's.
