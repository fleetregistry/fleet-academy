# Hire to Day One

This brings the major together. Each step is a course you've passed; this course is about doing them in order, with proof, and stopping wherever a person decides.

## The path
1. **Requirements.** Get the written must-haves for the role. None written: stop and ask. *Proof: the requirements.*
2. **Screen (RCT 110).** Every applicant against the must-haves, with evidence. Nothing about protected traits. *Proof: the screening table.*
3. **Interview kit (RCT 120).** Questions tied to the requirements, a scoring guide, the same for everyone.
4. **Schedule (RCT 210).** The required panel, in the candidate's time zone, invites kept separate.
5. **A person decides.** Gather the interviewers' scores into a summary for the hiring manager. **You don't pick the hire**, even if asked to "just take the top score". *Proof: the decision, from the hiring manager.*
6. **Offer draft (RCT 230).** From the approved offer only. Hand it to the approver; a person sends it.
7. **Signed acceptance.** Compare the signed offer with the approved one, term by term. If the candidate changed anything (pay, start date, title), stop: that's a new negotiation for a person and a new approval.
8. **Set up the record (HR 350, HR 310).** Enter the job details from the signed, matching offer: title, manager, department, location, start date. Pay and bank details are set up by payroll or HR, not you. *Proof: the record read back.*
9. **Conditions.** If the offer depends on a check (background, references, work eligibility), anything the rules tie to it, like accounts or the start date, waits until it clears. Tell the owner if that puts the start date at risk.
10. **Onboarding (HR 210)** and **training plan (LND 210).**
11. **Report.** To the owner: where the hire stands, what's waiting on whom. Record the chain (REC 101).

## Rules for the whole path
- Every step's proof goes in the hiring record, so anyone can follow the chain.
- If a step fails, go back to it. Don't skip ahead.
- Any concern a candidate or new hire raises about how they were treated goes to HR under HR 220.

## When to stop and ask
- No written requirements.
- The hiring decision: always a person.
- Offer terms not in the approval, or changed by the candidate.
- Anything that sets or changes pay.
- A condition hasn't cleared.
