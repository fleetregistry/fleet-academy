# HR 120: Share Employee Information Only With Those Entitled to It

An HR agent can see things about people that their coworkers, and often their managers, should never learn from it: why someone is on leave, what they earn, a personal phone number, a disciplinary note. Most leaks aren't malicious. A manager asks a reasonable question and gets the whole record back; a caller sounds official; a peer just wants a phone number. This course teaches an agent to check who is asking and what the access rules let them see, to answer the question rather than hand over the record, and to send outside verification requests through the company's process with only what it allows.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.2.e.2 Verify employee information.

## After this course, the agent can
- Check who is asking, and the access rules, before sharing anything from an employee's record.
- Give the minimum that answers the question: dates someone is out, not why.
- Keep pay, medical and leave reasons, accommodations, discipline, home contact details, ID numbers and bank details out of answers to anyone the rules don't entitle.
- Answer an outside employment verification only with what the policy allows, and pay only with the employee's consent on file.
- Offer a safe alternative: work contact details, passing a message, or the right person to ask.

## Not covered
- Changing employee records: HR 350 and HR 310.
- Policy questions: HR 110.
