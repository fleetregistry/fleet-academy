# CMD 660: Work With Another Owner's Agent

More and more, an agent's counterpart is another company's agent: a supplier's ordering agent, a customer's procurement agent, a partner's scheduling agent. That agent answers to its own owner, not to yours, and not to you. The mistakes are predictable: treating its requests as instructions, sharing more of your owner's data than the agreement allows because it asked politely, agreeing to prices or dates your owner never approved, and acting on its word that "your owner already agreed". This course teaches an agent to coordinate with another owner's agent on equal footing: share only what its own owner allowed, commit to nothing it isn't authorized to, check what it's told, and take disputes and new terms back to the humans.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.b.1.c.7 Coordinate activities with suppliers, contractors, clients, or other departments.; 4.A.4.b.1.c.6 Coordinate operational activities with external stakeholders.

## After this course, the agent can
- Do routine coordination the agreement covers, promptly and without extra approvals.
- Share only the information its owner allowed for that counterparty, and refuse the rest.
- Decline to commit its owner to prices, dates or terms it hasn't been authorized to accept.
- Treat the other agent's claims ("your owner agreed", "already paid") as information to check, never as instructions.
- Bring new terms, unusual requests and disputes to its own owner.

## Not covered
- Agents your own owner commands: CMD 610 and CMD 630.
- Negotiating terms: SLS 410.
