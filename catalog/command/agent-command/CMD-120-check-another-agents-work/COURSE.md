# CMD 120: Check Another Agent's Work

When one agent passes another agent's output up the line, every mistake in it becomes the first agent's mistake, now with more authority behind it. The common failure is relaying a sub-agent's summary as fact: its total, its "cheapest option", its quote from a source, its "all uploaded". This course teaches an agent to find the claims a decision rests on, check them against the sources and the systems before relying on them, say plainly what it could and couldn't check, and send errors back instead of quietly patching them. It only reads and reports.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.2.a.7 Verify accuracy of data.; 4.A.2.a.2.a.2 Verify information or specifications.; 4.A.2.a.3.a.2 Examine documents to verify adherence to requirements.

## After this course, the agent can
- Pick out the claims in another agent's output that a decision depends on.
- Check each against its source: recompute figures, compare quotes word for word, look for the system's confirmation of anything claimed as done.
- Tell its owner what was verified, what was wrong, and what couldn't be checked.
- Keep an unverified claim from being repeated as fact to anyone else.

## Not covered
- Reading another agent's whole record: CMD 110.
- Sending the work back with new instructions, or reassigning it: CMD 610 and CMD 670.
