# Check Another Agent's Work

Another agent's output is a claim until you've checked it. Once you pass it on, it carries your name too.

## 1. Find the claims that matter
Read the output and list the claims your owner's decision or next step depends on: the totals, the "best" or "cheapest" pick, the quoted figures, the dates, and every "done", "sent" or "uploaded". You don't need to check every sentence. You do need to check every claim that something rests on.

## 2. Check each against its source
- **Numbers:** recompute them from the source data. Look for what the summary left out: fees, taxes, setup charges, minimums, excluded rows. A ranking ("cheapest", "largest") is only as good as the totals behind it.
- **Quotes and figures from a document:** find the passage and compare it word for word, figure for figure. "The report says 12%" is wrong if the report says 2%.
- **"Done", "sent", "uploaded":** look for the system's own confirmation: a message ID, a file listing, a receipt. The agent saying so is not a confirmation.
- **Large outputs:** check every claim the decision rests on, and spot-check a sample of the rest. Say how many you sampled.

## 3. If you can't check it, say so
If you have no access to the source or the system, don't assume it's fine. Tell your owner the claim is unverified, what would verify it, and hold anything that would repeat it to someone else as fact (an email to a client, a report to a manager) until it's checked, or word it as unconfirmed.

## 4. Report what you found
Lead with the answer to the owner's actual question, corrected if needed: "Birch isn't the cheapest once its $450 setup fee is added; Cedar is." Then list:
- **Verified:** what matched, and against what.
- **Wrong:** what didn't, with the right figure and where it comes from.
- **Not checked:** what you couldn't verify, and why.

## 5. Send errors back; don't patch them quietly
If the other agent got something wrong, tell your owner and say what should go back to that agent. Don't silently fix its output and pass it on as if it had been right. The error, and the fact that it was caught, belong on the record.

## Don't
- Relay another agent's figure, ranking or "done" to anyone as fact before checking it.
- Change the other agent's work or its records. This course only reads and reports.
