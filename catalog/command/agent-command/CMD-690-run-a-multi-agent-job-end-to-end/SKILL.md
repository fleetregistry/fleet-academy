# Run a Multi-Agent Job End to End

Each step is a course you've passed. This one is about doing them in order, with proof, and stopping where a person must decide.

## The path
1. **Pin down the job.** What result, for whom, by when, and what the owner will do with it. Note the owner's rules for this job: what needs their yes, who else is in command of which agent, whether the owner reviews before anything leaves. If the goal or deadline isn't clear, ask now (CMD 210).
2. **Read the agents.** Check each candidate's record: proven skills on its current model, access, rules, current load (CMD 110).
3. **Plan the split.** Pieces as outputs, exact lists with no overlap, dependencies in order, each piece to an agent allowed to do it, you as the place it comes together (CMD 610). Give the owner the plan in a few lines.
4. **Brief each agent** with its piece, its limits and its stop points, passing down no more than you were given and no secrets (CMD 210, CMD 650).
5. **Watch the work.** Track each piece against its deadline. If an agent falls behind or fails, find out why before adding anyone (CMD 640); move work cleanly if you must (CMD 670).
6. **Check every piece before using it.** Recompute figures from their sources, compare quotes, look for the system's confirmation of anything "done" (CMD 120). Send wrong pieces back with what's wrong; don't patch them silently.
7. **Escalate what belongs to people** to the person in command, in one answerable message, and keep the rest of the job moving while you wait (CMD 630). Outside agents get only what the agreement allows (CMD 660).
8. **Put it together and check the whole** against step 1: everything asked for is there, consistent, and from checked pieces. Mark anything still unconfirmed.
9. **Deliver the way the owner said.** If they review first, deliver for review and send nothing onward.
10. **Report, record and close.** Lead with the result, then what needs the owner, what was corrected along the way, and anything unconfirmed (COM 101). Record the chain (REC 101). Ask for any access opened for the job to be removed (CMD 650).

## Rules for the whole job
- If a step fails, go back to it; don't skip ahead.
- One piece waiting on a person doesn't stop the others.
- Never claim the job done while any piece rests on an unchecked claim; say which.
