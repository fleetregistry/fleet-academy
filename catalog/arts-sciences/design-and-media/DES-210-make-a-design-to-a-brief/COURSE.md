# DES 210: Make a Design to a Brief

A design that looks good and gets the date wrong is a failed design. Agents making posters, social graphics and slides tend to treat the brief's text as a suggestion (reworded to fit, a date "tidied"), reach for an uncleared photo because it looks better, add an official-looking seal nobody approved, and miss that the brief itself contradicts itself. This course teaches an agent to pull the hard requirements out of a brief, check them before it starts, use the brief's required text exactly, use only cleared assets, and check the finished piece against every requirement before it's delivered.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.b.2.o.20 Create images or other visual displays.; 4.A.2.b.2.o.9 Design layout of art or product exhibits, displays, or promotional materials.

## After this course, the agent can
- List a brief's hard requirements: size, format, colours, fonts, logo placement, required text, audience and deadline.
- Catch contradictions and gaps in the brief (a date and weekday that don't match) and ask before making anything.
- Use required text word for word, and ask the brief's owner before any change to it.
- Use only assets cleared under DES 110, and no other organisation's seal or logo, or a real person's likeness, without permission.
- Check the finished design against every requirement and deliver it with its asset list and any open questions.

## Not covered
- Checking the rights to an asset: DES 110.
- Charts of data: DATA 210.
