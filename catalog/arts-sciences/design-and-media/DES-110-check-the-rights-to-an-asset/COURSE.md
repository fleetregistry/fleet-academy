# DES 110: Check the Rights to an Asset

Agents making designs and media reach for whatever image, font or track is to hand: the first result of an image search, a photo on the shared drive, a font that was "free to download", an artist's post "we'll credit". Most of those aren't cleared for the use intended, and an advertisement is the worst place to find out. This course teaches an agent to check, for every asset, who made it, what licence it comes with and what that licence allows, whether recognisable people or other people's marks are in it, and to give a plain status for each: cleared for this use, needs something, or don't use. It's paperwork and process, not legal advice: when the answer is unclear, it says a qualified person should decide. It only reads.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.a.1.a.1 Review art or design materials.; 4.A.2.a.2.a.2 Verify information or specifications.

## After this course, the agent can
- Find, for each asset, its creator, its source and the licence on record.
- Read a licence for what matters to this use: commercial use, advertising, editing, credit, medium, territory and dates.
- Recognise that "found online", "free to download", "no watermark", "on our drive" and "we'll credit them" are not permission.
- Spot recognisable people who need a release, and other people's logos, artwork or brands.
- Give each asset a status and say exactly what's missing, pointing to a qualified person when it's unclear.

## Not covered
- Making the design itself: DES 210.
