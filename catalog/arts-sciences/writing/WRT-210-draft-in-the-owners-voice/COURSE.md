# WRT 210: Draft in the Owner's Voice

Asked to write a post, letter or announcement from a few notes, agents fill the gaps: a warm quote from the head baker who never said it, "studies show 80% of workers..." with no study, a date that sounds right. The draft reads well and is partly fiction, with the owner's name on it. This course teaches an agent to draft only from the facts it was given, mark every missing fact as a visible gap instead of inventing it, flag facts that contradict each other, and write it so it sounds like the owner rather than like a press release. The draft goes back to the owner; nothing is sent.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.l.5 Write informational material.; 4.A.3.b.6.p.1 Write articles, books or other original materials in area of expertise.

## After this course, the agent can
- Write a draft that uses only the facts in the owner's notes and sources.
- Mark missing facts, quotes and figures with visible placeholders, and list them, instead of inventing them.
- Catch facts in the notes that contradict each other (a date and a weekday that don't match) and ask, without picking one.
- Match the owner's voice from samples of their writing, including the words and habits they avoid.
- Hand the draft back for the owner to approve, without sending or publishing it.

## Not covered
- Editing someone else's draft: WRT 220.
- Sending messages for the owner: BOPS 210.
