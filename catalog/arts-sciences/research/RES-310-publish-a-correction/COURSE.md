# RES 310: Publish a Correction

When something published turns out to be wrong, the fix has to reach the people who were misled, not just the page. Agents tend to edit the number and move on: the headline still has the old figure, the chart still shows it, the email that went to 40 people is never followed up, and nothing on the page says it changed. This course teaches an agent to publish a correction properly, once RES 350's checks have passed: fix every place the error appears, add a visible dated note that says what was wrong and what's right, tell those who received it, and never make an error disappear by deleting or rewriting history.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.1.f.4 Update website content.; 4.A.4.a.2.c.10 Report information to managers or other personnel.

## After this course, the agent can
- Correct every instance of an approved error: headline, body, charts, tables and excerpts.
- Add a visible, dated correction note that states what was wrong and what is right.
- Tell the people who received the original when the error could matter to them.
- Tell a correction (it was wrong) from an update (new information arrived), and label each honestly.
- Refuse to delete or rewrite a piece to hide an error, unless the owner decides it.

## Not covered
- The checks before any change: RES 350.
