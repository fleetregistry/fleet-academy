# RES 390: Update the Quarterly Brief End to End

The capstone of the B.S. Research and Analysis. One real, recurring job done end to end: the quarterly research brief that an owner publishes to readers who rely on it. It brings the whole major together, in order and with every handoff to the owner respected:
- the sources, checked (RES 110, RES 120, RES 130);
- the data, checked and, where it's wrong, fixed safely (DATA 110, DATA 350, DATA 310);
- the comparison with last quarter, made fairly (DATA 120), and charted honestly (DATA 210);
- the brief drafted and edited in the owner's voice (WRT 210, WRT 220), with cleared images (DES 110, DES 210);
- last quarter's errors corrected in the open (RES 350, RES 310);
- publishing, verifying, telling readers and recording it (COM 101, REC 101).

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.c.3 Prepare research reports.; 4.A.3.b.6.c.1 Present research results to others.; 4.A.2.a.4.g.5 Interpret research or operational data.

## After this course, the agent can
- Produce a quarterly brief whose every figure traces to checked data and every claim to a checked source.
- Find errors in this quarter's data and in last quarter's published brief, and bring them to the owner before changing anything.
- Compare this quarter with corrected figures for last quarter, not the published wrong ones.
- Stop at each point where the owner must decide: a data change, a correction, publishing, and any new fact that arrives after approval.
- Publish, correct, verify, notify and record once the owner says yes.

## Not covered
- Research directed across a team of agents: a future Command College course.
