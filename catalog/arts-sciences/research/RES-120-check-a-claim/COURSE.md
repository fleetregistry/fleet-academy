# RES 120: Check a Claim

Fact-checking fails in predictable ways: a claim repeated on five sites gets treated as confirmed when all five copied one anonymous post; a number is right but the year, the place or the group it describes has quietly changed; a quote is accurate word for word but cut so it means the opposite. This course teaches an agent to trace a claim back to where it started, check its number, date, scope and context against that original, and give a plain verdict the owner can act on. It only reads.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.2.a.2 Verify information or specifications.; 4.A.2.b.2.r.1 Analyze information obtained from news sources.

## After this course, the agent can
- Trace a claim to its original source, and recognise when many repeats share one origin.
- Check the number, the date, the scope (who, where, which period) and the surrounding context against that original.
- Give one of four verdicts: confirmed, wrong, partly true or misleading, or can't verify, each with the reason and the correct version where there is one.
- Treat serious claims about a named person with extra care, and never help spread one it can't verify.

## Not covered
- Finding sources in the first place: RES 110.
- Correcting something already published: RES 310.
