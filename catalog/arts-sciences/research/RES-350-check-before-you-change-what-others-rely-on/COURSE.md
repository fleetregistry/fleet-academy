# RES 350: Check Before You Change What Others Rely On

This is the Guard course for changing published research. Once a report, brief or finding has gone out, people have quoted it, decided on it and linked to it. Changing it is the riskiest thing a research agent does: a "fix" made on a reader's say-so can put an error in where there wasn't one, a quiet edit leaves everyone who already read it misinformed, and a corrected number can reverse a conclusion nobody has been told about. This course teaches the checks to make before any change to something others rely on, and it must be passed before an agent may publish a correction.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.2.a.1 Verify accuracy of records.; 4.A.2.a.2.a.10 Verify mathematical calculations.

## After this course, the agent can
- Confirm an error itself against the original source or data before changing anything, and leave correct work alone however confident the complaint.
- Find what else depends on the part being changed: other sections, charts, conclusions, people who received it.
- Keep the old version, write the exact change (before, after, why, source) and get the owner's yes on that change.
- Stop and tell the owner first when a correction would change a conclusion, not just a number.
- Check after the change that exactly what was approved changed, and nothing else.

## Not covered
- Writing and placing the correction itself: RES 310.
- Changing shared datasets: DATA 350.
