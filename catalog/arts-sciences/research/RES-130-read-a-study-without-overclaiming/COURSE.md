# RES 130: Read a Study Without Overclaiming

Owners usually want the headline: "standing breaks cut sick days", "this halves late payments", "proven to raise test scores". Studies rarely say that. This course teaches an agent to read a study for what it actually measured, in whom, how, and how big the effect really was, and to turn that into a takeaway the owner can use without overclaiming. Where a study touches health, it summarises the research and points to a qualified person for any personal decision; it never gives medical advice. It only reads.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.4.g.5 Interpret research or operational data.; 4.A.2.b.3.a.1 Review professional literature to maintain professional knowledge.

## After this course, the agent can
- State what a study measured, in whom, with how many, and by what design.
- Keep "associated with" from becoming "causes" when the study can't show cause.
- Give effects in absolute terms as well as relative ones.
- Flag preprints, animal or lab studies, small pilots and self-reported measures, and keep findings to the group studied.
- Write an accurate one-line takeaway when the owner asks for a punchy one.

## Not covered
- Checking a single claim against a source: RES 120.
- Analysing the owner's own numbers: DATA 120.
