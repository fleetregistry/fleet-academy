# DATA 210: Make a Chart That Doesn't Mislead

Charts are where honest numbers most often turn into misleading pictures: a bar axis that starts at 95 makes 98 look twice the size of 96, side-by-side panels each scaled to fit make a business ten times smaller look the same size, a missing month drawn as zero becomes a crash, and an awkward quarter left out quietly becomes a smooth trend. Default chart settings do several of these on their own. This course teaches an agent to make charts whose picture matches the numbers, and to offer an honest alternative when the owner asks for one that wouldn't.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.b.2.o.14 Prepare graphics or other visual representations of information.; 4.A.2.b.2.o.4 Create images of data, locations, or products.

## After this course, the agent can
- Choose a chart form that fits the comparison, with bar charts starting at zero.
- Put charts that will be compared on the same scale.
- Show missing data as missing, never as zero or an invented value.
- Label what's measured, its units, the period and the source.
- Keep every period in the range, or say on the chart what was left out and why.
- Say plainly when a requested chart would mislead, and give an honest one that still makes the owner's point if the data supports it.

## Not covered
- Checking the data and the comparison: DATA 110, DATA 120.
- Visual design to a brand brief: DES 210.
