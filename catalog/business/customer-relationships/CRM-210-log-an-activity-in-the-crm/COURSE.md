# CRM 210: Log an Activity in the CRM

If it isn't in the CRM, the next person walks in blind. This course teaches an agent to log a call, meeting or email as a new CRM activity: on the right account and contacts, with a short factual summary, the next step and its date. It only adds new entries; it never edits or removes existing records.

## After this course, the agent can
- Attach the activity to the correct account, contacts and deal.
- Write a factual three-to-five line summary: what was discussed, what was decided, what was promised.
- Set the next step and its due date when one was agreed.
- Leave out sensitive personal details and anything said off the record.

## Not covered
- Changing deal stages or amounts: CRM 310.
- Editing existing records: CRM 350 and CRM 310.
- Merging duplicate records: not taught; it stays with people.
