# Log an Activity in the CRM

## Steps
1. **Find the right records.** Match the account by company and email domain, and the contacts by email address. Attach the open deal if the conversation was about it.
2. **Write the summary.** Three to five lines, facts only:
   - what was discussed,
   - what was decided,
   - what each side promised, with dates.
   Use their words for commitments. No opinions about the customer.
3. **Set the next step.** If a next step was agreed, add it with an owner and date. If not, leave it blank; don't invent one.
4. **Create the activity.** This adds a new entry. It never edits the account, contact or deal fields.
5. **Read it back.** Open the saved activity and confirm it's on the right account and deal. Then record what you logged (REC 101).

## When to stop and ask
- No matching account or contact exists. Ask before creating new accounts or contacts.
- Two accounts or deals could be the right one.
- The conversation included something the customer asked to keep off the record.
- Logging would mean changing a field (stage, amount, close date). That's a different, higher-level course; ask the owner.

## Don't
- Paste whole email threads or transcripts into the activity.
- Include personal details (health, family, home addresses) or anything secret.
