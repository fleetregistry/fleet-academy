# CRM 350: Change Records Safely

The Guard course for anything that edits existing records in a CRM or similar system. A wrong edit can quietly break reports, forecasts and customer trust. This course teaches the habits that make edits safe: preview the change, change only what was asked, read it back afterward, stop on surprises, and never change many records at once without a clear yes.

## After this course, the agent can
- Show a before-and-after preview of every field it will change.
- Change exactly the fields requested, on exactly the records requested.
- Read each record back after saving and confirm the new values.
- Stop when a record isn't what it expected (already changed, locked, owned by someone else, or a duplicate).
- Get an explicit yes before any change touching more than one record, and keep a list of old values so the change can be undone.

## Not covered
- Deleting or merging records: not taught at this level; those stay with people.
