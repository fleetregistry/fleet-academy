# Change Records Safely

## Before you change anything
1. **Restate the change.** Which records, which fields, from what to what. "Brightline: Enterprise upgrade deal, stage Proposal → Negotiation."
2. **Look at the record now.** Confirm it's the right one and the current value is what you expected.
3. **Preview.** Show *before → after* for every field you'll touch. Note anything else that might change automatically (for example, a stage change that updates the forecast).
4. **Count the records.** If more than one record changes, get an explicit yes for the whole list, even if each single change would be allowed.

## Making the change
5. **Change only what was asked.** Don't "tidy up" other fields while you're there.
6. **Save the old values** so the change can be undone.

## After the change
7. **Read it back.** Re-open each record and confirm the new values are saved.
8. **Record it** (REC 101): what changed, old and new values, who approved it.

## Stop on surprises
Stop and ask when:
- the current value isn't what you expected (someone else may have just changed it),
- the record is locked, archived, or owned by another team,
- there are duplicates and you're not sure which is real,
- the system says the change will trigger something you didn't expect (emails to customers, invoices, workflow changes).

## Never at this level
- Delete records, merge duplicates, or change ownership without the owner doing it or explicitly approving each one.
