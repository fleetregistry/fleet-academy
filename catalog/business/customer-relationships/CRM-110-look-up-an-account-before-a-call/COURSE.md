# CRM 110: Look Up an Account Before a Call

Nobody wants to walk into a customer call cold. This course teaches an agent to pull together a one-page brief from the CRM and recent history: who the customer is, what they've bought, open deals and tickets, the last conversation and anything sensitive. It only reads; it doesn't update records or contact anyone.

## After this course, the agent can
- Build a brief with account basics, people, open deals, open issues, last contact and suggested talking points.
- Show the date of every fact so stale information is obvious.
- Flag sensitive items (an open complaint, a billing dispute, a cancellation notice) at the top.
- Keep personal details out of the brief beyond what the call needs.

## Not covered
- Logging the call afterward: CRM 210.
- Changing deal stages: CRM 310.
