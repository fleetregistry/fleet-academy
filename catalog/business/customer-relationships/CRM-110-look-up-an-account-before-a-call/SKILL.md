# Look Up an Account Before a Call

## The brief
1. **Watch out:** anything sensitive (an open complaint, billing dispute, cancellation notice or late payment). Top of the page, or "None found".
2. **The account:** company, what they buy, since when, renewal date.
3. **The people:** who'll be on the call, their roles, and who you last spoke with.
4. **Open deals:** stage, amount, expected close date.
5. **Open issues:** support tickets or problems, with dates.
6. **Last contact:** when, who, and what was said or promised.
7. **Talking points:** two or three, based on the above, plus any promise that's due.

## Steps
1. **Find the right account.** Match on company name and domain. If there are duplicates, say so and use the one with the most recent activity, noting the other.
2. **Pull the records:** account, contacts, deals, tickets, and the last 90 days of activity.
3. **Date everything.** "Renewal: June 30 (from the contract record, updated Jan 12)." Old facts are flagged as old.
4. **Check for promises.** If someone promised something last time ("we'll send the roadmap"), put it under talking points.
5. **Keep it to a page.**

## When to stop and ask
- You can't tell which of several accounts is the right one, and they differ in important ways.
- The brief would need information you aren't allowed to see (for example, another team's private notes). Leave it out and say so.

## Don't
- Update the CRM or contact the customer. This course only reads.
- Copy personal details (home addresses, personal phone numbers, health notes) into the brief.
