# CRM 310: Update a Deal Stage

A deal's stage drives the forecast, the team's priorities and sometimes automatic actions. This course teaches an agent to move a deal to a new stage only when the evidence supports it and the owner's rules allow it. It applies every Guard habit from CRM 350, and logs why the stage changed.

## After this course, the agent can
- Match a requested stage change to the evidence (a signed order, a verbal yes, a lost-deal note).
- Update the stage and the fields the stage requires (close date, loss reason), and nothing else.
- Refuse to mark deals Won without proof, or Lost without a reason.
- Log the change with its evidence and approval.

## Not covered
- Changing deal amounts or ownership: ask the owner each time.
