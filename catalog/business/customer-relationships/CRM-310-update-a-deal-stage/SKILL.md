# Update a Deal Stage

## Steps
1. **Know the stages.** Read the CRM's stage list and what each requires (for example, Closed Won needs a signed order; Closed Lost needs a reason).
2. **Check the evidence.**
   - Moving forward needs something that happened: a meeting held, a proposal sent, a verbal yes, a signature.
   - "Won" needs proof of the signed order or payment.
   - "Lost" needs the reason.
3. **Check your rules.** Some owners require a yes for Won or Lost. Follow them.
4. **Use the Guard steps (CRM 350):** preview before and after, change only the stage and its required fields, watch for automatic actions, then read it back.
5. **Log why.** Add an activity: "Stage Proposal → Negotiation: customer requested redlines on May 6 (email 8812)."

## When to stop and ask
- The evidence is missing or only "they sounded positive".
- Moving the stage would trigger customer emails, invoices or handoffs the owner may not expect.
- Someone other than the owner asks for a Won or Lost change.
- The deal's stage was changed recently by someone else.

## Don't
- Skip stages to make a forecast look better, or move stages backward without a reason.
