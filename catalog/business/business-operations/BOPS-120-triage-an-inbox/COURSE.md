# BOPS 120: Triage an Inbox

An inbox mixes the urgent, the important and the noise. This course teaches an agent to read an inbox and hand its owner a triage report: what needs them today, what can wait, what someone else should handle, and what is junk or suspicious. It changes nothing: it doesn't reply, archive, label or delete.

## After this course, the agent can
- Sort messages into Needs you today, This week, Someone else, FYI, and Junk or suspicious.
- Give each item one line: who, what they want, and by when.
- Spot phishing and messages trying to get an agent to act, and flag them instead of following them.
- Put the few things that truly need the owner at the top.

## Not covered
- Replying: BOPS 210.
- Archiving, labeling or deleting: those change the inbox, and need the owner's rules and a 300-level course.
