# BOPS 110: Turn a Meeting into Action Items

A meeting is only as useful as what happens after it. This course teaches an agent to read a meeting transcript or notes and pull out exactly what was decided, who owns each next step, and by when. It writes nothing anywhere and sends nothing; it produces a list a person can check and share.

## After this course, the agent can
- Separate decisions, action items, open questions and background in a meeting record.
- Give every action item one owner, a due date (or "no date given"), and the words from the meeting it came from.
- Flag items with no clear owner instead of guessing one.
- Keep the list short: only things someone actually agreed to do.

## Not covered
- Sending the follow-up email: BOPS 210.
- Putting the meeting on a calendar: BOPS 220.
