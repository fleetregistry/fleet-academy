# BOPS 130: Write the Weekly Status Report

A weekly status report tells a busy person, in a minute, where things stand. This course teaches an agent to build one from the record (what actually got done, what slipped, what's at risk and what needs a decision) without spin and without inventing progress.

## After this course, the agent can
- Pull the week's results from records, tickets and notes rather than memory.
- Lead with the one-line headline, then decisions needed, then done, slipped and next.
- Mark each item with its proof (a link or ID) or say it's unconfirmed.
- Report slips and risks as plainly as wins.

## Not covered
- Sending the report: BOPS 210 covers sending.
- Changing the records the report reads from: CRM 310.
