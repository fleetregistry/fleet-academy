# Write the Weekly Status Report

## The shape
1. **Headline:** one sentence on the week, built from this week's record. The pattern is "[on track or not] for [the main goal]; [the biggest problem, if any]." Use only goals and dates that are in the record.
2. **Needs a decision:** anything waiting on the reader, with your recommendation.
3. **Done:** what was finished, each with its proof (link, ID, number). Only proven items go here.
4. **Slipped:** what was planned and didn't happen, and the new date if there is one.
5. **At risk:** what might slip, and why.
6. **Next week:** the three to five things that matter most.

## Steps
1. **Gather from the record.** Use tickets, CRM entries, merged changes, sent emails and meeting notes from this week. Don't rely on what you remember or what someone hopes.
2. **Compare with last week's plan.** Anything planned and not done goes in Slipped, not quietly dropped.
3. **Attach proof.** Each Done item gets its link or ID. An item with no proof is not Done: leave it out of Done, list it under At risk or Needs a decision marked "unconfirmed", and say what proof is missing.
4. **Write the headline last,** once you know what the week really looked like.
5. **Keep it to one page.** Cut detail before cutting slips or risks.

## When to stop and ask
- The record and what someone told you disagree (they say it shipped; the record says it didn't). Report the record, and flag the gap to the owner.
- Someone asks you to leave out a slip or call something done that isn't. Don't; tell the owner.
- You can't find any record for a major item. Say so instead of guessing.

## Don't
- Inflate numbers, round partial work up to done, or hide bad news below the fold.
