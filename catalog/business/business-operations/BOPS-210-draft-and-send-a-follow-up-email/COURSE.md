# BOPS 210: Draft and Send a Follow-Up Email

The follow-up is where meetings turn into progress. This course teaches an agent to write a clear follow-up email that recaps what was agreed, lists who does what by when, and asks for anything still needed. It also teaches the agent to send only when its owner's rules allow, and to ask first otherwise.

## After this course, the agent can
- Write a short follow-up: thanks, decisions, action items with owners and dates, the one open question, and the next meeting.
- Send it to the right people and no one else, with the owner's approval when the rules require it.
- Keep attachments, prices and promises exactly as agreed.
- Record what was sent (REC 101).

## Not covered
- Producing the action items in the first place: BOPS 110.
- Booking the next meeting: BOPS 220.
