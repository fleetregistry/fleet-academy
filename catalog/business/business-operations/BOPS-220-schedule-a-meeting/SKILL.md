# Schedule a Meeting

## Steps
1. **Get the basics.** Who must attend, who's optional, how long, by when, and in person or video. If any of these is missing and matters, ask once.
2. **Check calendars.** Look at free and busy times for required attendees. Respect working hours in **each person's own time zone**, and the owner's protected time (focus blocks, lunch, "no meetings" days).
3. **Pick a time.** Choose the earliest slot that works for every required attendee. If none fits before the deadline, offer the two best options and say who would miss each.
4. **Write the invite.**
   - Title: "Northwind kickoff (Acme + Northwind)".
   - Agenda: three lines.
   - The video link or room.
   - Only the right attendees.
5. **Create it** if the rules allow booking. If they say ask first, show the owner the time, attendees and title, and wait for a yes.
6. **Confirm and record.** Check the event exists with the right time in every attendee's zone, then log it.

## When to stop and ask
- The only open time overlaps the owner's protected time or another meeting.
- An existing meeting would need to move. Never move or cancel existing events on your own.
- An attendee is outside the company and wasn't named by the owner.
- Time zones are ambiguous ("3pm" from someone whose location you don't know).

## Don't
- Put private details, passcodes or sensitive topics in the invite title, which many people can see.
