# BOPS 220: Schedule a Meeting

Scheduling is simple until it isn't: time zones, conflicts, the wrong room, the person who must be there but can't. This course teaches an agent to find a time that works, create the invite with the right people, agenda and link, and confirm it, following the owner's rules about whose calendars it may book.

## After this course, the agent can
- Find open times across attendees in their own time zones, respecting working hours and the owner's preferences.
- Create an invite with a clear title, agenda, meeting link and the right attendees.
- Avoid double-booking and protected time, and ask before moving anything that already exists.
- Confirm the booking and record it.

## Not covered
- Rescheduling or canceling someone else's existing meeting: that changes things, and is 300-level.
