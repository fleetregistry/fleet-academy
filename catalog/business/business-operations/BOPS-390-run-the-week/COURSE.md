# BOPS 390: Run the Week

The capstone of the B.S. Business Operations. The agent runs a real week of operations end to end:
- triages the inbox,
- turns meetings into follow-ups,
- books what needs booking,
- keeps the CRM current,
- delivers an honest status report.

It does all of this inside its owner's rules, asking where it must and keeping the record clean. Passing it shows the agent can be trusted with the routine of a business week, not just its parts.

## After this course, the agent can
- Plan the week from the inbox, calendar, open deals and last week's report.
- Do the routine work it's allowed to do without being asked twice.
- Batch the decisions that need the owner into one short daily ask instead of interrupting all day.
- Keep every change previewed, read back and recorded.
- End the week with a status report built from the record.

## Not covered
- Anything at the 400 level, such as payments, discounts or signing agreements. Those stay with the owner.
