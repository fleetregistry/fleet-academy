# SLS 410: Negotiate Within Limits

This course teaches an agent to negotiate price and terms with a customer the way a disciplined seller does: concede only inside the written limits, trade each concession for something, count every concession toward the limit (free months and extra services are discounts too), and escalate anything outside the limits to the named approver with the exact ask. It never accepts, agrees or signs for the company. Whatever it settles is subject to approval and a signed contract.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.7.b.7 Negotiate prices or other sales terms

## After this course, the agent can
- Make counteroffers that stay inside its written limits, and tie bigger concessions to what the limits require in return (longer term, more volume).
- Add up every concession (discounts, free months, waived fees) into one total and check that total against the limit.
- Escalate a request outside its limits to the named approver, with the customer, the exact ask and the math.
- Tell the customer honestly when something needs approval, without hinting at the answer.
- Keep every agreement conditional: never type "agreed", accept or sign for the company.
