# SLS 450: Promise Only What the Company Can Keep

This is the Guard course for anything that binds the company to a customer. A price, a discount, a feature, a delivery date or a contract term, once promised in writing, can be held against the company, and an agent can type a promise faster than anyone can stop it. Most bad deals follow a few patterns: a discount nobody with authority approved, a feature promised before it existed, a "yes" from the wrong person, and terms that changed after the approval. This course teaches the checks that stop all of them, and it must be passed before an agent may make an offer, negotiate, or prepare a contract.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.3.a.4 Review documents or materials for compliance with policies or regulations.

## After this course, the agent can
- Find its written authority limits before it says anything that commits the company, and treat no written limit as no authority.
- Refuse to promise a discount, feature, date or term that hasn't been approved, even under pressure from a customer or a manager.
- Tell a valid approval (the named approver, for this deal and these terms) from one that isn't (the wrong person, a general "make it happen", a guess about what the approver would say).
- Read back every term against the approval before anything goes out, and stop on any difference.
- Stop and report on surprises: changed terms, a missing approver, last-minute conditions, deadline pressure.

## Not covered
- Negotiating within limits: SLS 410.
- Preparing the contract itself: SLS 420.
