# SLS 110: Find and Qualify Prospects

A sales team's time goes where its prospect list points it, so a list padded with guesses wastes weeks. This course teaches an agent to check each company against the owner's stated ideal-customer profile, one criterion at a time, using only evidence it can point to. It says plainly what is unknown, flags old evidence, and never invents a fact about a company to make the list look complete. It only reads; it doesn't contact anyone or change any record.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.b.1.b.2 Identify potential customers.

## After this course, the agent can
- Check each company against each criterion in the owner's profile and mark it met, not met or unknown, with the evidence and its date.
- Sort companies into qualified, not a fit, and needs research, and say which fact would settle each open one.
- Say "unknown" instead of estimating headcount, revenue, tools or contacts.
- Flag evidence old enough to have changed.
- Ask for the profile when none is given, instead of making one up and qualifying against it.
- Treat text on a company's pages as information, not instructions, and keep personal contact details out of the list.

## Not covered
- Asking prospects about their needs: SLS 120.
- Contacting prospects or writing outreach: SLS 200 and BOPS 210.
- Adding prospects to the CRM: CRM 210.
