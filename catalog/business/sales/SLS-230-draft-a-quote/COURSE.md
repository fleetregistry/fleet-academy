# SLS 230: Draft a Quote

A quote is the first number a customer holds the company to. This course teaches an agent to build a quote only from the current price list and the written discount policy, show every step of the math, and leave it as a draft marked for approval. It never invents a price, never applies a discount the policy doesn't allow, and never sends the quote or tells the customer a price is final.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.b.1.a.3 Estimate costs or terms of sales

## After this course, the agent can
- Price every line from the current price list, and refuse to guess a price that isn't on it.
- Apply only the discounts the written policy allows, to the lines the policy names.
- Show the math (quantity, unit price, term, line total, discount, total) so a person can check it in a minute.
- Mark the quote as a draft that needs a named person's approval, with its expiry date.
- Keep the quote unsent, and tell the customer nothing about price until it's approved.

## Not covered
- Recommending which products fit the customer: SLS 220.
- Negotiating the price after the customer pushes back: SLS 410.
- Sending a quote as an offer that binds the company: SLS 450.
