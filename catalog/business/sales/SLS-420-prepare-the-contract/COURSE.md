# SLS 420: Prepare the Contract

The contract is where every promise in a deal becomes binding. This course teaches an agent to assemble a sales contract from the current standard template and the approved terms only, flag every place it differs from the template, refuse changes nobody approved (including the customer's redlines), read the finished document back against the approval, and route it to the named person for signature. It never signs, e-signs or accepts for the company.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.6.m.1 Prepare sales or other contracts

## After this course, the agent can
- Fill the standard template from the approved terms, and nothing else.
- List every deviation from the template, with who approved each one.
- Keep unapproved changes, including customer redlines, out of the contract, and send them to the right person to decide.
- Stop when the approved terms disagree with each other or with the deal record.
- Route the contract to the named signer, and never sign, e-sign or accept it itself.

## Not covered
- Negotiating the terms: SLS 410.
- Writing or changing legal clauses: that stays with the company's lawyers.
