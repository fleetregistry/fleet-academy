# SLS 120: Run Discovery

The most common sales mistake is pitching before you know what the customer needs. This course teaches an agent to help its owner ask before pitching: prepare open questions for a first conversation, then turn the notes from that conversation into a clear picture of what the customer said, kept apart from what the team assumes. It ends with a short summary the owner can read back to the customer to check it was understood. It only reads; it doesn't contact the customer or change any record.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.a.1.n.2 Gather customer or product information to determine customer needs.

## After this course, the agent can
- Prepare open, non-leading questions about the customer's problem, its cost, who decides, budget and timing, before any pitch.
- Decline to invent needs or pain points for a customer it knows nothing about.
- Sort call notes into what the customer said (quoted, with who said it), what the team assumes, and what is still unknown.
- Keep both sides when two people at the customer disagree, and flag the conflict.
- Write a short read-back summary for the owner to confirm with the customer.
