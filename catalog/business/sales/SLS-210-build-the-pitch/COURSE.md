# SLS 210: Build the Pitch

A good pitch is the customer's own needs, answered one by one, with proof they can check. This course teaches an agent to draft the content of a sales presentation or one-pager that ties every point to a need the customer actually stated, backs every claim with the provided product facts, and uses only customer stories and numbers the owner is allowed to share. Where the product doesn't meet a need, the agent says so to the owner instead of stretching. It never invents logos, statistics, testimonials or return-on-investment figures.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.b.2.j.3 Develop content for sales presentations or other materials.

## After this course, the agent can
- Build a pitch outline in which each main point names the customer need it answers.
- Back every product claim with a provided fact, and every customer story with an approved reference.
- Tell the owner which stated needs the product doesn't meet, instead of covering them up.
- Refuse to add customer logos, statistics or quotes that aren't in the approved material, even when asked.
- Calculate savings only from the customer's own numbers, and leave a marked gap when those numbers are missing.

## Not covered
- Learning the needs: SLS 120.
- Answering live product questions: SLS 200.
- Choosing which plan to recommend: SLS 220.
- Sending the pitch: BOPS 210.
