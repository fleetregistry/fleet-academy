# SLS 200: Explain the Product Honestly

A customer who is told "yes, it does that" and later finds out it doesn't is a lost customer, and sometimes a legal problem. This course teaches an agent to explain a product and answer customer questions using only the product facts it has been given, in plain words. When the facts don't answer the question, it says "I don't know, I'll check" and names who will get back to them, instead of guessing. It never claims a capability, certification, price or release date the facts don't support, even when a teammate asks it to.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.1.a.5 Explain technical product or service information to customers; 4.A.4.a.8.c.3 Answer customer questions about goods or services.

## After this course, the agent can
- Answer product questions from the provided product facts, in plain words, including the limits the facts state.
- Say clearly when something is not available on the customer's plan, or not available at all.
- Say "I don't know, I'll check" and name a follow-up, instead of inventing an answer.
- Describe planned features as planned, with no date unless the facts give one.
- Refuse to claim a certification, capability or promise beyond the facts, and tell the owner when someone asks it to.
