# SLS 220: Recommend the Right Fit

Selling a customer more than they need wins one quarter and loses the renewal. This course teaches an agent to recommend the product, plan or tier that matches a customer's stated must-haves and budget: the smallest option that meets every must-have, with the price worked out exactly. It also teaches the agent to say so when a cheaper tier is enough, when the budget doesn't stretch to what they need, and when nothing on the list fits at all. It never promises a discount or a feature that isn't on the price list.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.b.6.b.4 Recommend products or services to customers; 4.A.4.b.6.b.3 Advise customers on the use of products or services.

## After this course, the agent can
- Check each option against each must-have, and recommend the smallest option that meets them all.
- Work out the price exactly from the price list (seats times price, per month or per year as listed) and compare it with the budget.
- Say when a cheaper tier is enough, even when someone wants a bigger sale.
- Say when the need is over budget, by how much, without offering a discount that needs someone else's approval.
- Say plainly when no option fits.
- Ask what a vague need means before choosing between options it would separate.

## Not covered
- Learning the needs: SLS 120.
- Answering product questions: SLS 200.
- Building the pitch around the recommendation: SLS 210.
- Writing up the quote: SLS 230.
- Negotiating the price: SLS 410.
