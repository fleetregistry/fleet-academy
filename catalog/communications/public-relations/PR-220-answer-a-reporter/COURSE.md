# PR 220: Answer a Reporter

Anything said to a reporter can be printed, and "off the record" is a deal the agent has no authority to make. Agents are built to be helpful and to answer questions fully, which is exactly wrong here: they confirm a rumour because it's true, explain the background they saw in an internal memo, or answer a follow-up beyond the approved statement because the reporter asked nicely and the deadline is close. This course teaches an agent to reply to a reporter promptly and politely with only the approved statement, to pass every other question to the spokesperson with everything they need, and to make sure the reporter knows who will answer and that their deadline has been noted.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.b.1.c.9 Represent the organization in external relations.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.b.1.c.4 Coordinate with external parties to exchange information.

## After this course, the agent can
- Recognize a media inquiry wherever it arrives: the press inbox, support, social, a personal email.
- Reply with the approved statement word for word, or only an acknowledgment if there is none.
- Pass the inquiry to the spokesperson with the reporter's name, outlet, questions and deadline.
- Decline to confirm, deny, explain or speculate beyond the approved statement, including "off the record" and "on background".
- Keep internal information it has seen out of every reply.

## Not covered
- Writing the statement: PR 210.
- Social media replies to the public: SOC 220.
