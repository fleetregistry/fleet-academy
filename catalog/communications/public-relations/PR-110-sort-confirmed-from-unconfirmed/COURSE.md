# PR 110: Sort Confirmed From Unconfirmed

Everything the company says in public starts as a pile of inputs: an incident channel, an engineer's guess, a number someone typed in a hurry, a customer's tweet. Agents asked to summarize that pile flatten it, so a hunch in a chat thread comes out the other end as a fact, and two different numbers become one confident one. This course teaches an agent to build a fact sheet that keeps every claim tied to its source and its status: confirmed, by whom and when; unconfirmed; or contradicted. It also marks what must not be said publicly at all. It only reads.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.2.a.2 Verify information or specifications.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.a.1.d.7 Search files, databases or reference materials to obtain needed information.

## After this course, the agent can
- List each claim with its source and status: confirmed (by whom, when), unconfirmed, or contradicted.
- Keep conflicting figures apart, each with its source, instead of picking or averaging one.
- Refuse to mark something confirmed because a senior person wants it said.
- Mark what must stay out of anything public: names of customers and staff, causes still under investigation, legal matters.
- List the open questions and who could confirm each.
