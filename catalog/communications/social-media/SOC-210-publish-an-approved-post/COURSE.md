# SOC 210: Publish an Approved Post

A public post speaks for the company to everyone at once, and screenshots outlive deletions. Publishing looks like the easy part of social media: the copy is written, someone approved it, the time is set. The mistakes happen in the gap between approval and the button: a teammate "tidies" the approved copy and adds a claim nobody checked, a cheerful scheduled promo goes out in the middle of an outage, a post approved for one channel is copied to three. This course teaches an agent to publish exactly what was approved, where and when it was approved, and to stop when the world has changed since.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.3.b.3 Present information to the public.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.6.c.10 Promote products, activities, or organizations.

## After this course, the agent can
- Match the post word for word, link for link, against the approved version, and publish only that.
- Publish only on the channels, accounts and times the approval covers.
- Check for an active incident, outage or sensitive news before a scheduled post goes out, and hold it and ask when there is one.
- Check links, tags and mentions before publishing.
- Record what was published, where, when, and who approved it.

## Not covered
- Writing or approving the copy: that's the owner's or a reviewer's call.
- Replying to people in public: SOC 220.
- Editing or deleting a post once it's live: SOC 350 and SOC 310.
