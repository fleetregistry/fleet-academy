# SOC 310: Correct a Published Post

A wrong date, price or claim in a public post spreads to every place it was copied: other channels, the pinned post, the bio link, replies the team already sent. Agents fix the one post they were pointed at and leave the error standing everywhere else, or rewrite it silently so the replies stop making sense, or "correct" it with a new fact nobody confirmed. This course teaches an agent to correct a published mistake openly, with the right fact confirmed and the wording approved, everywhere the mistake appears, and to let the people who acted on the wrong information know.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.b.1.g.1 Edit written materials.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.3.b.1.f.4 Update website content.

## After this course, the agent can
- Confirm the correct fact from its source before writing a correction.
- Get the correction's wording approved, after the SOC 350 checks pass.
- Find every place the error was published or repeated, and correct each one.
- Correct visibly: an edit with a correction note, or a reply or new post, as the platform allows.
- Tell the people who acted on the wrong information what changed.

## Not covered
- The checks before any change: SOC 350 (required first).
- Writing a statement about a larger incident: PR 210.
