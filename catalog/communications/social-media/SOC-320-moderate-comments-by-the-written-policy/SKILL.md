# Moderate Comments by the Written Policy

You're removing other people's words from public view. Do it only for a written reason.

## 1. Have the policy in front of you
Your owner's written moderation policy says what may be hidden or removed, and whether to hide (only the poster and their friends see it) or delete. If there's no written policy, don't moderate: report what you'd remove and why, and ask.

## 2. Sort each comment
For every comment you'd act on, name the exact rule it breaks. Common ones:
- **Spam or scams:** links to unrelated sites, fake giveaways, impersonating the company.
- **Private data:** card numbers, addresses, phone numbers, account details, whoever posted them, including the customer about themselves. Hide it, even when it's also a complaint, then tell the person privately (SOC 220) that it was hidden to protect them and how to reach support about their problem. Never repeat the data itself, in your message or in your log: write "a card number", not the number.
- **Abuse the policy names:** slurs, harassment of a named person.
- **Threats of harm** to anyone: don't just delete. See step 4.

## 3. Criticism stays
Complaints, angry reviews, sarcasm, "your product broke", "worst service ever", and criticism of the company or its leaders are **not** reasons to hide or remove, however much a colleague wants them gone. Route them for a reply instead. If someone asks you to remove comments for being negative, say no, name the policy, and offer to flag the ones that need an answer.

## 4. Threats and safety
A threat of violence, or a report of someone in danger, goes to the person your rules name (security, safety, or your owner) at once. Save it first: the text, the account, the link and the time. Hide it only if the policy says to, and only after it's saved. Never delete the only copy.

## 5. Before any removal
Run the SOC 350 checks: the policy or the right person authorizes this action, a copy is kept, nothing is under a hold, one comment at a time.

## 6. Record it
For each comment you hide or remove: the link, the account, the rule it broke, what you did (hidden or deleted), when, and where the copy is (REC 101). Also list the comments you left up that need a reply.
