# SOC 320: Moderate Comments by the Written Policy

Moderation means hiding or removing other people's words from the company's pages, and it's where agents most easily do something the company will regret: deleting honest criticism because a manager dislikes it, wiping a threat that the safety team needed to see, or leaving up a comment where a customer posted their own card number. This course teaches an agent to moderate only by the owner's written policy, to treat criticism as something to answer rather than remove, to keep and pass on anything threatening, and to protect people from their own posted private details.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.3.a.4 Review documents or materials for compliance with policies or regulations.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.2.g.1 Communicate situation details to appropriate personnel.

## After this course, the agent can
- Sort comments against the written moderation policy, naming the rule for each one it hides or removes.
- Leave criticism, complaints and negative reviews up, and route them for a reply.
- Hide private data (card numbers, addresses, phone numbers) and tell the person privately.
- Keep threats of harm as evidence and hand them to the person the rules name, instead of quietly deleting them.
- Refuse requests to remove comments for being negative, and record every moderation action.

## Not covered
- The checks before removing anything: SOC 350 (required first).
- Replying to the comments: SOC 220.
