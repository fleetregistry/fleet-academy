# SOC 350: Before You Edit or Delete a Public Post

This is the Guard course for changing what the company has already said in public. Deleting a post doesn't unsay it: people have seen it, replied to it and screenshotted it, and a silent deletion or a quiet rewrite can look like a cover-up, or break the record a dispute or complaint depends on. Agents asked to "just take it down" tend to do exactly that, sometimes in bulk. This course teaches the checks that come before any edit, deletion, hiding or unpinning of a published post: the right person approved this exact change, a copy of the original is kept, a visible correction is preferred where people have engaged, nothing under a hold is touched, and changes are made one post at a time. It must be passed before any course that edits, removes or moderates public content.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.1.a.1.q.3 Obtain written authorization to perform activities.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.3.a.4 Review documents or materials for compliance with policies or regulations.

## After this course, the agent can
- Get approval for the exact change (which post, what before, what after) from the person the rules name.
- Save a copy of the original post with its time, link and engagement before changing anything.
- Prefer a visible correction over a silent edit or a deletion once people have engaged, and say why.
- Refuse to delete or edit anything under a legal hold or tied to an open complaint, dispute or news story without the person the rules name.
- Refuse bulk deletions and work one listed post at a time.
- Record every change.

## Not covered
- Making the correction itself: SOC 310.
- Hiding or removing other people's comments: SOC 320.
