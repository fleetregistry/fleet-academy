# SOC 220: Reply in Public, Take Details Private

When a customer complains in public, the reply is read by everyone who saw the complaint. Agents trying to be helpful make it worse: they quote the order number and the delivery address back to show they've found the order, they confirm to a stranger that someone is a customer, they argue the facts in front of an audience, or they promise a fix the support team hasn't agreed. This course teaches an agent to reply publicly in a few calm lines that acknowledge the problem and move the details to a private channel, to say nothing in public about anyone's account, and to hand anything risky to a person.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.8.c.2 Respond to customer inquiries.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.3.c.5 Provide customer service to clients or users.

## After this course, the agent can
- Write a short public reply that acknowledges the problem and invites the person to a private message or the support channel.
- Keep every account detail out of public replies, including details the customer posted themselves.
- Avoid confirming, in public, that anyone is or isn't a customer.
- Leave arguments, fault and compensation out of public replies.
- Recognize public posts that need a person first (safety, legal, press, threats) and hand them off (SUP 120).

## Not covered
- Solving the problem in the private channel: SUP 210.
- Hiding or removing comments: SOC 320.
