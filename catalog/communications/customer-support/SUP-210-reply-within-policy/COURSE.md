# SUP 210: Reply Within Policy

A support reply speaks for the company, and customers keep it. The common failures aren't rudeness; they're kindness. An agent wants to help an upset customer, so it promises a delivery date the tracking doesn't support, waives a return window nobody waived, or makes up a rule where the policy is silent. This course teaches an agent to write a warm, clear reply that answers every question the customer asked, says only what the written policy and the order record support, keeps any promise a colleague already made, and says plainly when something needs a person's decision.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.8.c.2 Respond to customer inquiries.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.8.c.6 Correspond with customers to answer questions or resolve complaints.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.1.b.6 Communicate with clients about products, procedures, and policies.

## After this course, the agent can
- Read the ticket history before replying, and keep what a colleague already promised.
- Answer every question in the message, not just the first.
- State dates and amounts exactly as the record shows them, as estimates when they are estimates.
- Decline what the policy doesn't allow, kindly, without inventing an exception, and say who can decide.
- Say "I'll find out" instead of inventing a rule when the policy doesn't cover the case.

## Not covered
- Deciding whether a ticket is the agent's at all: SUP 120.
- Issuing refunds or credits: SUP 450 and SUP 410.
- Replying in public on social media: SOC 220.
