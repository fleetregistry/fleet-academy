# SUP 490: Resolve a Complaint End to End

The capstone of the B.S. Communications and Customer Support. One real complaint, taken from the moment it appears (often in public) to a customer who has what the policy owes them and knows it, with every step done to standard:
- deciding whether it's the agent's to handle at all (SUP 120);
- a public reply that moves it private, if it started in public (SOC 220);
- a private reply that answers everything within policy (SUP 210);
- the refund or credit, safely and once (SUP 450, SUP 410);
- closing the loop with the customer and on the record (COM 101, REC 101).

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.8.c.4 Communicate with customers to resolve complaints or ensure satisfaction.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.8.c.1 Respond to customer problems or complaints.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.1.c.5 Issue money, credit, or vouchers.

## After this course, the agent can
- Carry a complaint from first sight to resolution without skipping a check or a required approval.
- Notice, at any step, a signal that takes it out of the agent's hands, and hand off cleanly.
- Keep every public word free of account details and every private promise inside the policy.
- Issue what's owed once, confirm it, and tell the customer exactly what will happen.
- Close the ticket only when the customer has what they were owed, with the proof on the record.

## Not covered
- Changing the policy, or granting exceptions: a person's call.
- Incidents affecting many customers at once: PR 110 and PR 210.
