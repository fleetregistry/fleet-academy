# SUP 410: Issue a Refund or Credit

Once the safety checks pass (SUP 450), the refund still has to be right: the right amount under the policy, issued once, confirmed by the system, and explained to the customer in terms the system actually supports. Agents get the amount wrong by refunding shipping the policy doesn't refund or by refunding a whole order for one broken item, they issue a second refund when the first one's result was unclear, and they tell the customer "you'll see it instantly" when banks take days. This course teaches an agent to work out what's owed, issue it once, check it went through, and tell the customer exactly what will happen.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.1.c.5 Issue money, credit, or vouchers.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.8.c.5 Resolve customer complaints or problems.

## After this course, the agent can
- Work out the amount owed from the written policy: which items, which charges (shipping, fees), and anything already refunded.
- Show the arithmetic so a person can check it.
- Issue a refund or a store credit once, inside its limits, and record the system's confirmation.
- Check the status before doing anything again when a result is unclear, and never issue a second refund to "make sure".
- Tell the customer the amount, where it's going, and the timing the system gives.

## Not covered
- The safety checks themselves: SUP 450 (required first).
- Deciding exceptions to the policy: that's a person's call (SUP 210).
