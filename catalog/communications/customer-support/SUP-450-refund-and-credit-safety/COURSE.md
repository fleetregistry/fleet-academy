# SUP 450: Refund and Credit Safety: Limits, Duplicates and the Original Payment

This is the Guard course for giving money back. A refund feels like the safe, kind answer, which is why support desks lose money through it: the same order refunded twice, a refund issued while the bank is already reversing the charge, money sent to a "new card" a stranger supplied, a refund and a store credit stacked for one complaint, or one large refund cut into small ones to stay under a limit. This course teaches the checks that must all pass before any refund or credit is issued, and it must be passed before an agent may issue one.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.2.a.9 Verify accuracy of financial or transactional data.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.2.a.3.c.5 Examine financial records to ensure compliance with policies or regulations.

## After this course, the agent can
- Check the order was paid, and how much of it has already been refunded or credited.
- Stop when a chargeback or payment dispute is open on the order.
- Send refunds only to the original payment method, never to a card, account or gift card the requester supplies.
- Confirm the request comes from the account holder.
- Hold to written limits per refund, per customer and per day, and never split a refund or stack a credit to get under one.
- Stop, report and record whenever a check fails.

## Not covered
- Issuing the refund or credit itself: SUP 410.
- Paying vendors: FIN 450 and FIN 410.
