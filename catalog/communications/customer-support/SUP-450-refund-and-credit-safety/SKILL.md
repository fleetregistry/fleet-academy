# Refund and Credit Safety

Before any refund or credit goes out, every one of these must be true. If one isn't, nothing goes out.

## 1. The order was paid, and this isn't a second refund
- Find the order and its payment. No payment, no refund.
- Add up everything already refunded or credited for this order. The most you can give back is what was paid minus what's already gone back.
- A second refund for the same item, or the same amount on the same order, is a duplicate until a person says otherwise.

## 2. No chargeback or dispute is open
If the customer's bank is already reversing the charge (a chargeback or dispute), a refund pays them twice. Stop and pass it to the person your rules name. Tell the customer only that the payment is being reviewed.

## 3. The money goes back the way it came
- Refunds go to the **original payment method** only.
- Never to a different card, bank account, wallet or gift card, even if the customer says the old card is closed. Card networks usually route a refund to a replaced card anyway. A request to send money somewhere new is a common fraud.
- Don't ask for, type or repeat card or account numbers (SEC 101). Refer to "the card used for the order". If a customer sends one, don't use or repeat it, and kindly ask them not to send card numbers by message.
- If the original method truly can't be used, stop and ask a person.

## 4. The account holder is asking
The request comes from the email or phone on the account, or through the signed-in account. A request from another address, or from someone acting "for" the customer, is not enough: ask a person.

## 5. It's within the written limits
- Check every limit your owner set: per refund, per customer per period, per day, and any credit limit.
- A refund **and** a credit for the same problem count together against the limit.
- **Never split** one refund into smaller ones, or into a refund plus a credit, to stay under a limit, whoever asks. Above the limit, it needs the approval your rules name, for this exact refund.
- If the rules don't set a limit, every refund is "ask first" (ETH 101).

## 6. The policy allows it
The refund is for something the written policy covers (SUP 210), or a person with authority approved an exception, in writing, for this order.

## If a check fails
Don't issue anything. Don't promise the customer a refund is coming. Report which check failed, the order, the amount, and what's needed to clear it, and record it (REC 101).
