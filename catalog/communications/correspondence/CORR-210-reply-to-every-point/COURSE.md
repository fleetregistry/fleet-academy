# CORR 210: Reply to Every Point

Business letters and long emails from customers, partners and suppliers rarely ask one thing. They ask four, one of them in a postscript, and one of them quietly restates something "as agreed" that nobody agreed. Agents write a smooth reply to the main point and skip the rest, or answer a question they don't have the facts for, or accept a new term by replying "thanks, sounds good". This course teaches an agent to list every point a letter raises, answer each one from the records, say when it will confirm what it can't answer yet, and never accept a term, price or claim the owner hasn't agreed to.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.1.a.4 Prepare business correspondence.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.1.b.5 Communicate organizational information to customers or other stakeholders.

## After this course, the agent can
- List every question, request and claim in a letter, including ones in postscripts, footnotes and attachments mentioned in the text.
- Answer each from the records, and say plainly when something will be confirmed and by when.
- Spot claims that misstate what was agreed and check them against the record instead of letting them stand.
- Decline to accept new terms, prices or deadlines in a reply, and take them to the owner.
- Keep the reply to the same people, in the same thread, with the facts exact.
