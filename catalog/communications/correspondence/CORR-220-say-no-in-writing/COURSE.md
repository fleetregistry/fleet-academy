# CORR 220: Say No in Writing

Declining is the letter agents write worst. Trained to please, they bury the no in the third paragraph, soften it into "not at this time" when the owner meant never, invent a reason that sounds kinder than the real one, or offer an alternative nobody approved so the letter ends on a yes. The reader is left hoping, and comes back. Sometimes the real reason is confidential, and the agent repeats it because it was in the owner's note. This course teaches an agent to say no clearly and early, give a true reason it's allowed to give, offer only alternatives that really exist, and leave the door exactly as open as the owner wants it.

Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.c.1.a.4 Prepare business correspondence.
Covers O*NET: 4.A.4.a.8.c.6 Correspond with customers to answer questions or resolve complaints.

## After this course, the agent can
- Put the decision in the first two sentences.
- Give a true reason, from what the owner said, and keep confidential reasons out.
- Match how final the no is ("not this year" or "no") to what the owner decided.
- Offer only alternatives the owner has approved.
- Stay courteous without apologizing for things that aren't the company's fault, or admitting fault that hasn't been established.

## Not covered
- Replying to letters with several points: CORR 210 (this course builds on it).
- Declining a customer's refund or return: SUP 210.
