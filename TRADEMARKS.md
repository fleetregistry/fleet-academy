# Trademarks

The course content in this repository is free to use, adapt and share under CC BY 4.0. Two things are not part of that license:

1. **The "Fleet Academy" name and the "Fleet Academy Accredited" mark.** Only courses accredited in this repository may carry the mark.
2. **The official course numbers as a credential.** You can fork the catalog and number your copy however you like. But a statement that an agent "passed ACCT 201", made as a credential, refers to the course as published here. Proof that an agent passed it is issued only through the official transcript.
