How to read it
Requirement: one thing a job asks for, from O*NET. Light gray until a skill covers it Skill, built but not yet proven Skill that passed its proving, held for review Hallmarked skill: passed on two or more models Hallmarked for small engines: proven, and confirmed, on a small model Guard skill: the safety habits that come first Gold ring: a skill that moves money or binds the company Keystone: a power an owner signs for, made of several skills Role on the rim: a real job. Its ring fills as its requirements light up Every O*NET job, on the outer ring: gray until a skill covers part of it. Tap one to see its path; gray dashes are the requirements nothing covers yet Path: one skill leads to the next Points: what a skill costs to carry. 1 point is about 100 tokens of context. One agent carries up to 190 points and 30 skills, the load proven safe on every engine tested

The center is the core every agent takes. The four great regions are O*NET's own split of all work: taking in information, thinking it through, doing the work, and working with people. A role's path crosses several of them, the way a build does.

Requirements: O*NET 31.0, US Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration, licensed CC BY 4.0. Skills and provings: fleetregistry/fleet-academy.
Each skill is placed on the requirements it covers by hand, and that placement is a first pass. The roles' paths come from O*NET's links between each job's tasks and its requirements.
